Decision Lens Prototype

Classify the judgment, score the lenses, get a structured recommendation.

This prototype treats decisions as four different workflows: observing an ongoing process, deciding on a threshold, checking whether a process is ripe for a decision, and evaluating the regime that followed a past decision. No backend, no persistence, just a decision aid.

1. What kind of judgment is this?

Do not start with abstract scoring. First decide what sort of thing you are evaluating. The wrong mode produces the wrong judgment.

2. Which domain are you working in?

Domain choice does not change the engine. It changes the labels, examples, and the way the recommendation reads.

3. Score the relevant lenses

For each lens, provide your estimate, how confident you are in that estimate, and how important that lens is in this case.

4. Recommendation

The prototype returns a state, main drivers, uncertainty, and what would most improve clarity.
