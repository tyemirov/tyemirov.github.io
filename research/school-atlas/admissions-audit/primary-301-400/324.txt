Student Enrollment
Whether you're joining us for the first time or returning for another year, all students are required to complete the registration process. This ensures access to student fees, course registration, school technology, and essential services.
To begin the process of attending NTHS, please review the following steps:
In order to enroll at New Trier Township High School, students must live within district boundaries on a full-time permanent basis. The District 203
attendance area
includes Glencoe, Kenilworth, Wilmette, Winnetka, and portions of Northfield, Glenview, and Northbrook.
Throughout the school year, the
Registrar’s Office
supports transfer students and incoming freshmen and their families as they transition to New Trier Township High School. Families are encouraged to call (847) 784-2212 or email
enrollment@nths.net
for assistance.
Registration Process
Incoming Freshmen Enrollment
To begin the registration process, you are required to complete a pre-registration form. Please read below and choose the category that best fits your student.
This applies to students from our sender schools as well as those coming from other educational settings, including out-of-district, private, parochial, and homeschool environments.
Click here to start the registration process
Grade 9-12 Transfer Student
Your child is in grade 9, 10, 11 or 12 and you have moved within the District 203 attendance area.  This includes students who transfer to New Trier High School from an Illinois public school, a private or parochial school, a school outside of Illinois or a school outside of the U.S.
Click here to start the registration process
Returning Student
Your child was enrolled as a New Trier High School student and is returning for the upcoming school year.
Click here to start the registration process

Student Fees
All newly enrolled students are required to pay applicable fees prior to the start of the academic term. These fees cover essential services such as registration, technology access, and student activities.
To view the full breakdown of student fees and make a payment, Click
here
.
IEP/504, Special Education
If your student has an IEP or receives special education services, you will need to consult with the Director of Special Education prior to taking our placement test.  Please contact:
Megan Zajac
Director of Special Education

ESL Services
We are committed to supporting multilingual learners and their families. Our ESL services provide language development support, academic assistance, and resources to help students thrive in an inclusive learning environment. To learn more about available programs, eligibility, and how to get started, please contact the ESL department.
