New Families Start Here
Looking to register at your home boundary school? Start with #1 below. Interested in a school that is not your home boundary school? Start with #2 below.
* Note: Open enrollment applications must be approved prior to registering for a school that is not your home school. If you are registering for your home boundary school, you DO NOT need to fill out an open enrollment application.
Chrome is the preferred browser for registering students.
New Parents:
For parents new to CUSD (grades Preschool-12),
click here to begin your child's registration process
for your attendance boundary school. Registrations are being accepted for the current school year, 2026-27.
Open Enrollment:
Families interested in their child attending a school other than their attendance boundary school will need to
apply for open enrollment
. Open enrollments are being accepted for the current 2025-26 school year, and the upcoming 2026-27 school year.
If applying for an open enrollment school, please do not complete your child's registration for the Open Enrollment school
UNTIL
you have received your approval email or letter.
Chandler Unified looks forward to welcoming your students!

Documents Needed
Household information – address and phone numbers
Parent/Legal Guardian information – work and cell phone numbers, e-mail addresses
Student information – demographic and health information
Emergency contact –  names and phone numbers
*Proof of Residency – current utility bill, lease agreement, etc. (see Acceptable Proof of Residency under Additional Student Registration Information on the
District Enrollment Page
)

If you have questions, please contact the attendance boundary school.

Open Enrollment Capacity
Open Enrollment enables Arizona students to attend public schools outside their attendance area. In accordance with state law, the District has established an open enrollment policy and implemented an open enrollment program without charging tuition for non-resident students and resident transfer students. Open Enrollment applications may be submitted online (elementary) or via email or in-person at our junior high and high schools.
Per district policy 5-105 and 5-105A and ARS 15-816, acceptance of a student's Open Enrollment will be based on site capacity, the students' prior discipline, grades and/or attendance. Please note that the district does not transport open enrollment students.
Open Enrollment capacity data is updated every 12 weeks per legislative requirements.
Capacity may change prior to data being updated.
