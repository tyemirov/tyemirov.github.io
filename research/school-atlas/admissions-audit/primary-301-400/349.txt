New Student Registration
Welcome new Longhorn families!
If you are a new student (
not currently enrolled in Forsyth County Schools
) enrolling at Lambert High School, please set up a registration appointment at the Hill Center at this link first:
https://www.forsyth.k12.ga.us/district-services/technology-information-services/information-systems/registration-home/new-student-registration
Once you’ve completed your registration appointment at the Hill Center, a Lambert counselor will reach out to you!
Prior to setting up an appointment with a Lambert Counselor, please complete the following 2 steps:
1.)Obtain a copy of your students transcript or report card to assist with class scheduling
2)Review the course registration guide and appropriate grade-level worksheet below
