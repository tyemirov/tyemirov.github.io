New Student Registration
New Student Registration
We are excited to have your family join our school district.  Enrollment in Weston Public Schools is contingent upon residency.  You
MUST
be a resident of Weston to attend Weston Public Schools.  Before a student can be registered and begin school, parents or legal guardians must provide proof of residency.  Because residency may change for students, Weston Public Schools will continually verify residency.
Your child’s registration will not be considered complete until all documents have been approved by the Registrar.
If you prefer to bring documents in person to the registrar at Weston High School, you may do so by APPOINTMENT ONLY.
Please email all documents to the Registrar,
Christina Jolly
Required Documents:
New Student Registration Form
If you are registering multiple children, you will need to create a separate packet for each child.  Should you need translation services, please make an appointment with the registrar’s office.

Pre-K Registration
Pre-K Weston Windows Registration
Weston Windows, our integrated preschool program for children ages 3 years to 5 years old, provides a comprehensive range of services within a structured, supportive and engaging preschool environment. Weston Windows welcomes all children.  To be considered for our preschool program, parents must sign their student(s) up for our waitlist by completing the
Weston Windows Waitlist Application Form
Please use the following link to register your student:
https://www.westonschools.org/resources/new-student-registration
, as well as the
Weston Windows Parent Questionnaire
.
More information can be found on our
Weston Windows Integrated Preschool Program
website.
Kindergarten Registration
Kindergarten Registration
If your child turns 5 prior to September 1, 2026, your child is eligible for Kindergarten. For more information regarding our Kindergarten program please visit our
Kindergarten Information
website.
As we prepare for the transition to Kindergarten, please be aware that if your child is enrolled in the Weston Windows program it will not transfer to the Kindergarten program.
Because Preschool and Kindergarten registration systems are separate, you will need to complete a new registration for your child to enroll in Kindergarten.
We recommend beginning the registration process as soon as possible to ensure a smooth transition for the upcoming school year.
To enroll in Kindergarten, please submit the documents listed in the New Student Registration tab to the Registrar,
Christina Jolly
. Your child’s registration will not be considered complete until all documents have been approved by the Registrar.
Please note, Weston Public Schools has two lower elementary schools: Country and Woodland. Your school and class assignment will be emailed to you at the end of August.

Withdrawals
While we hope that all of our students complete their education with us, we understand that circumstances may require students to unenroll.  Any student leaving Weston Public Schools must
click here to complete the Withdrawal Form

In order to complete the withdrawal process and have your transcript/school records forwarded to your new school, it is necessary that you return all books and District property, and pay any fees owed.

Due to the realities of homelessness and mobility, students in transition may not have enrollment documents available. Nonetheless, the district will immediately enroll any child or youth in transition. Enrollment may not be denied or delayed due to lack of any documentation normally required for enrollment.
