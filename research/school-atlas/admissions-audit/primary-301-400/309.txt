High School Registration
(updated as of 1/9/2026)
REGISTRATION DATES
Registration appointments for new students for 2026-2027 school year will be held between
March 9
and
April 10, 2026
.
Summer registration will take place between
July 6 and July 17, 2026 by appointment with the SPHS registrar
.
NEW STUDENTS
Registration occurs in a four-step process completed by the parent or legal guardian of the child:
Verify your residency in South Pasadena and collect your three proofs of residence.
Register online via the
Aeries Internet Registration system (AIR)
. Please visit the
https://southpasadenausd.aeries.net/enrollment/
to complete the online registration process, and print out two copies of your completed AIR registration.
After you complete the online registration, and you have all the required documents, email Kathryn Pistello (
[email protected]
) to schedule an appointment for registration. To double-check that you have all the correct materials, please print and read the documents under
Forms/Files
found in the right-side navigation menu: HS Registration Required documents, and Enrollment Registration Checklist.
Attend a registration appointment at South Pasadena High School, 1401 Fremont Avenue, South Pasadena, CA 91030. Please bring to your appointment the following original documents:
Copy of your AIR registration
Birth certificate or passport of student
Student immunization records
Unofficial transcript (if out of country, a translated English version)
CAASPP scores (if available)
Special Circumstances (English as a second language, special education, health concerns)
Three required proofs of residency. See acceptable proofs of residency in the column at the right.
If your student is a current 8th grader at South Pasadena Middle School, they will automatically be enrolled at South Pasadena High School for 9th grade for the following school year. The first day of school for the 2026-2027 school year will be Wednesday, August 12, 2026.
NOTES:
Guardianships and Dual Family Residencies must be approved in advance by the Human Resources office at the District Office. Please email Katy Nielsen at
[email protected]
South Pasadena High School closes after Summer School ends on July 17, 2026, and will reopen on
August 3, 2026.
PREVIOUSLY WITHDRAWN STUDENTS FROM SPUSD
If you are a South Pasadena resident, and your child previously attended a SPUSD school, you do not need to complete the online registration. Email Kathryn Pistello (
[email protected]
) to schedule an appointment and indicate the last school, grade and year he/she attended in SPUSD.
Please bring an unofficial transcript from the previous school. Transcripts for 9th grade students must show both 7th and 8th grade. Withdrawal grades are required if transferring during the school year. If entering from another country, an official transcript with a notarized English translation is required. If there are special circumstances (ESL, Special Education, or health problems) please provide appropriate documentation, such as ELPAC test scores or current IEP or 504 plan.
Residency Verification Documents - one document from EACH category, and all documents must be originals, no copies accepted.
Category A
Deed of Trust
Mortgage Payment Receipt – dated within the past month
Rental/Lease Agreement,
listing all names of occupants (including children)
, and the name, address, telephone number and signature of landlord/management company.
Property Tax Bill – dated within the past year
Closing Escrow Papers
Category B
NOTE:  All bills must be both parts, top & bottom, in English, with all pages present, i.e, if a six page gas bill,  please present entire bill. Online bill pay allows for downloadable pdfs of entire gas and electric bills.
Utility Bill (Example: Southern California Edison, The Gas Company, Athens Services, Water Bill) – dated within the past month
Cable/Internet Provider Bill (Example: Spectrum, Direct TV, DISH)  – dated within the past month
Category C
NOTE:  All bills must be both parts, top & bottom, in English, with all pages present, i.e, if a six page gas bill,  please present entire bill. Online bill pay allows for downloadable pdfs of entire gas and electric bills.
An additional Utility Bill [different than what is provided in CATEGORY B]   (Example: Southern California Edison, The Gas Company, Internet or cable provider, Athens Services, Water bill)   – dated within the past month
