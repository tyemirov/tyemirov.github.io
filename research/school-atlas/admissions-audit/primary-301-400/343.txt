Residency
In accordance with Illinois School Code and Board Policy 7:60- Residence, only students who reside within the attendance boundaries of Community High School District 128 may enroll at Vernon Hills High School or Libertyville High School. During the registration and enrollment process, families will be asked to provide the residency documentation listed below. To streamline the process, the District contracts with a third-party vendor to electronically verify student address information. At any point in this process, families may be asked to provide additional documentation.
Required Residency Documents (Provide One):
Current
rental or lease agreement
OR
Mortgage statement, loan document, or closing statement
Supporting Residency Documents  (Provide Two):
Documents must be dated within the last 60 days.
Water bill
Electric bill
Gas bill
Cable bill
Car insurance or vehicle registration
Homeowner's or Renter's Insurance
Public Aid Card
KidsCare Medical Card
With any questions related to the enrollment or residency verification process, please contact your building's registrar or visit the registrar's websites:
LHS
and
VHHS
.
