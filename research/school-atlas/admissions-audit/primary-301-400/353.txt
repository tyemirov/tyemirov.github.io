Enroll in MTSD
We are excited to welcome you to the Mequon-Thiensville School District! Our district serves students in grades 4K-12 in the communities of Mequon and Thiensville, north shore suburbs of Milwaukee that embrace world-class learning opportunities with rich arts, athletic and extracurricular opportunities for students.
The following information provide answers to frequently asked questions regarding enrolling in MTSD. If you have any additional questions, please contact the office of student services at
262-238-8510
or
register@mtsd.k12.wi.us
.
Steps to Enroll a New Student at MTSD

Collapse All
Step 1: Verify Residency
All families, including those that already have children enrolled in the district, are required to verify residency when enrolling a new child.
Children who are residents of Mequon or Thiensville are entitled to attend the Mequon-Thiensville schools. In order for us to do the best job possible, it is important that we verify student residency. This page explains how you can establish, for our records, that your child is a resident of the City of Mequon or Village of Thiensville. Proof of residency will be required prior to registration in our district.
If you are a resident of Mequon or Thiensville and your child lives with you, the following documents can be used to establish your child’s residency. Students who qualify for admission to District schools, per Board Policy 5111, will be accepted when the following documents have been submitted:
An original birth certificate or passport;
Proof of residency consisting of: a property tax bill, a current signed rental agreement, or a closing statement.
For Home Owners:
Closing Statement OR Mortgage Statement OR Property Tax Bill; AND
Utility Bill within the last 30 days or insurance statement
For Renters:
Current Signed Lease/Rental Agreement; AND
Utility Bill within the last 30 days or insurance statement; AND
Completed Lease/Renter Verification Form
An Immunization Record; and
If applicable, court orders, legal custody documentation, and/or placement papers.
If you cannot furnish any of these documents because you live with a resident of Mequon or Thiensville, other documents to establish your child’s residence will be required.
If you have any questions about this required information, our processes or the intent of this information, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Step 2: Provide a Birth Certificate & Proof of Immunizations
Birth Certificate
Please note that either Michelle Halloran or your child’s school office staff will need to view and verify your child’s original birth certificate (or passport) prior to the start of the school year.
Immunizations
Families must submit legally mandated immunization records. Records can be submitted anytime prior to the start of the school year (or within 30 days of the start of school) and do not need to be submitted during pre-registration. Learn more about immunization requirements at.

After the Residency and Birth Certificate have been verified, you will receive a link to complete your online registration through Infinite Campus.
4K & 5K Program Information
Expand All
Collapse All
4-year-old Kindergarten
We offer a tuition-free, half-day kindergarten for four-year-olds, five days per week at our three elementary schools—Donges Bay, Oriole Lane, and Wilson—as well as one morning section with community partner Range Line Preschool and Childcare. Classes run Monday through Friday from 9 a.m.-11:45 a.m. (morning session) or 1:15-4 p.m. (afternoon session).
Morning and Afternoon Placements & Lotter
Families indicate their morning or afternoon program preference during pre-registration. Student placements in morning or afternoon sections will be made according to family preference whenever possible. We retain the right to make final decisions on placements.
If more families indicate a preference for a morning or afternoon section than we have available seats, we will enact a formal lottery system, which is the most equitable approach to situations in which capacity is limited. Registrations received after May 1st will not be included in the lottery.
Families may still enroll at any time after the May 1 pre-registration deadline, however, those families will not participate in the class preference lottery.
5-year-old Kindergarten
Children in MTSD's 5-year-old kindergarten program (5K) experience a high-quality early-learning program that enhances our young learners' early understanding of literacy, numeracy and social-emotional learning. Passionate and dedicated teachers nurture children's innate curiosity as they prepare students for a promising future.
MTSD's 5K is a full-day program; children
must be at least five-years-old on or before September 1 of that school year
in order to enroll.

Enrolling as a Non-Resident at MTSD
The Mequon-Thiensville School District participates in the
Wisconsin Interdistrict Open Enrollment Program
, which allows parents to apply for their children to attend school districts other than the one in which they reside. Wisconsin residents in K4 through Grade 12 may apply to participate in the Open Enrollment program. Parents must submit an application form to nonresident school districts during a window of time that generally begins with the first full week in February and runs through the last week in April. (Please visit the
Department of Public Instruction website
for exact dates and times.)
Each year the MTSD Board of Education meets to determine whether seats will be available and, if so, which grade levels will have openings. If there are more applicants than there are seats available, we engage in a computerized random selection process to determine accepted applicants.

How Many Seats are Available for Non Resident Students?
Each year the MTSD Board of Education meets to determine whether seats will be available and, if so, which grade levels will have openings. If there are more applicants than there are seats available, we engage in a computerized random selection process to determine accepted applicants. Seat selection is
not
based
on academic performance or extra-curricular activities. There is a preference given in the lottery for students that already have siblings in the district.
Even if there are no seats available in your grade, you are encouraged to apply and be put on a wait list. There is a possibility that additional seats may be allocated later in the summer. If seats become available, we will notify those families on the waitlist.
Enrollment for the 26-27 school year is now closed.  No additional seats are being offered to those already on our waitlist.
For the 26-27 school year, the district set the following open enrollment seats:
2 seats in Grade 5
1 seat in Grade 4
0 seats in special education
0 seats in the speech/language program

The Mequon-Thiensville School District coordinates the selection process for students through the use of a computerized program. Students are randomly selected into the Open Enrollment program.

What school will my child attend?
The Mequon-Thiensville School District serves the residents of the City of Mequon and Village of Thiensville. Our district consists of three elementary schools serving students in 4K-5th grade, two middle schools serving students in 6th-8th grade, and one high school serving students in 9th-12th grade. If your child will be enrolled in 4K-8th grade, please see our attendance maps to learn in which elementary or middle school attendance zone you reside. All students in 9th-12th grade attend Homestead High School. If you have any questions or require assistance, please contact the student services office at 262-238-8510.

Is the process for enrolling a student in 4K different than other grades?
Families of students entering 4-year-old kindergarten and families of kindergarten (5K) students who are NEW to MTSD are asked to pre-register their child in addition to other enrollment procedures.
The online pre-registration window is February 14 through May 1 annually.
Pre-registration allows us to develop class rosters and determine staffing to meet projected enrollment. Through pre-registration, families will select their preferred 4K morning or afternoon program option.
