What is the registration process?
A parent/guardian must first complete the online registration form for the appropriate school year and provide the required documentation. The required documentation can be uploaded to the online registration, email to Amelia Dougherty (
amelia.dougherty@rtsd.org
) directly, or submitted in-person at the Administration Building (135 S. Wayne Ave Wayne PA 19087). Once a registration is submitted and reviewed, Amelia Dougherty will email a confirmation that your child will be fully enrolled or if any additional documentation will need to be submitted. Once your child is fully enrolled, either the Main Office or the Guidance Office of your child’s enrolling school will contact you with the next steps.
I completed the online registration, can I just stop by?
No, The registration process can be completed online by uploading the required documents or emailing them directly to Amelia Dougherty, Central Registrar at
amelia.dougherty@rtsd.org
. If you would like to submit the documents in person, please make an appointment. Appointments must be made at least 24 hours in advance to submit documents in person due to the high level of received registrations and appointment requests.
Can I mail/email/fax my registration information or just show up to drop it off?
Registration documents can be uploaded to the online registration or emailed. They can also be submitted in person by making an appointment.
How long will my registration appointment take?
Registration Appointments can take anywhere from 10 minutes to an hour depending on how many children you are registering at one time and if you are also completing the registration as well.
Do I need to bring my child to the registration appointment?
No, you do not need to bring your children with you to registration appointment.
I don't have a computer or internet access. How can I complete the online registration?
Please call the Central Registration office at 610-688-8100 ext. 6100 for assistance.
I live in the district but don't have a lease or mortgage. Can I still register?
There are questions within the registration portal that will allow you to provide more information. You may have rights under the McKinney-Vento Law. Learn more about Education for Homeless Youth in Radnor Township School District by clicking this
link
.
I am moving within the district; how do I update our address?
All matters regarding residency and updating addresses start with Central Registration. Updated proofs of residency will need to be submitted in order to update your address. The updated proofs of residency can be emailed to Central Registration or submitted in person at the Administration Building.
My child(ren) are moving/transferring out of Radnor Township School District Schools. How do I Withdrawal them?
The withdrawal process starts with completing the RTSD Withdrawal Request Form found
here
. Central Registration will contact you upon submission of your child(ren)’s withdrawal notice for confirmation and any follow up questions.
I'm moving into Radnor Township in the summer. Can we register our child to start in September?
Yes. Once you establish residency in Radnor Township, please complete the online registration form and make an appointment with our Central Registrar. The registration office is open year-round.
What is the deadline to register?
There is no deadline to register, the registration office is open year-round.
I have other children in Radnor Schools, do I still need to prove residency?
Yes. For every new student registered to attend Radnor schools, a parent/guardian must complete the registration process and provide all required proofs of residency and other documents.
Does Radnor Township School District have a full day Kindergarten program?
Yes, Radnor Township School District kindergarten students are enrolled in a full day program.
What is the required age for entrance to Kindergarten?
The student must be 5 years old on or before September 1 of their kindergarten year.
What are the immunization requirements for Radnor Township School District?
The immunization requirements for PA students are available on the
Health Services website
.
When will I know the school bus details for my child?
Bus assignments will be shared with all qualifying families from our Transportation department before the start of the school year. Questions regarding transportation should be directed to our Transportation office at 610-293-1947.
Where is the Radnor Township School District Central Registration office?
Central Registration is located in the Radnor Township School District Administration Building (135 S. Wayne Avenue Wayne, PA 19087).
I have a question about registration that I did not see on this FAQ list. Who do I ask?
Please contact Central Registrar Amelia Dougherty at 610-688-8100 ext. 6100 or
amelia.dougherty@rtsd.org
.
Radnor Township School District offers a remote registration process for students entering the district.  To register, parents/guardians must complete registration online for each student joining the District and submit the required documents.

Required Documents
1. Child(ren)'s Proof of Age - one (1) of the following:
Original Birth Certificate
Valid Passport
Baptismal certificate
Copy of the record of baptism, notarized or duly certified and showing date of birth
Notarized statement from the parents/guardians or relative indicated date of birth
Prior school record indicating date of birth
Note: Kindergarten students must be 5-years-old on or before Sept. 1 of the student's first school year. 1st-grade students must be 6-years-old on or before Sept. 15.
2. Immunization Record
Student's Immunization Record
Written statement from prior school district or medical office that the required immunizations have been administered, or that a required series is being administered
Verbal assurance from prior school district or a medical office that required immunizations have been completed, with records to follow
3. Proof of Residency*
One (1) of the following
:
A Deed
A Lease
​​​​​​​
Two (2) of the following showing registrant's name and address (cannot be two of the same):
Valid driver’s license or change of address card
Valid vehicle owner’s card
Utility bill within 30 days
Paycheck stub within 30 days
Most recent tax bill
Most recent sewer bill
*Other forms of proof of residence may be acceptable. The District will conduct due diligence to confirm authentication and residence but will not use multiple forms of documentation to delay enrollment.
