Enrollment
Thank you for your interest in enrolling your student at Highland Park High School.
To begin enrollment, please click
here
.
Students must live within the district boundaries of HPISD in order to attend our school.  If you are planning a move to our district you can click
here
to check a specific address in
SchoolSite Locator
, an interactive attendance boundary locator, to verify if an address is located within HPISD attendance boundaries.
If you have questions regarding enrollment at Highland Park High School, please call the Counseling Office at  (214) 780-3773.
Enrollments are made by appointment only, no walk-in appointments can be accommodated.
HPHS CAMPUS TOURS
Highland Park High School offers two official group tours during each school year. Tours occur in the afternoon outside of school hours and are
open to current 8th - 11th grade prospective students and their parents
who will be new to HPISD for the 2027-28 school year
.
The Winter Tour will be Wednesday, December 2 from 4:00-4:45 p.m.
To sign up, fill out the form.
The spring tour is typically in late March, 2027.
