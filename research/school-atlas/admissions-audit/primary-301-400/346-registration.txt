Welcome to the
Northern Valley Regional High School 
 Student Application/Registration Process
Northern Valley Regional High Schools serve residents of the following New Jersey towns for students in grades 9 - 12:
Northern Valley Demarest High School:  Demarest, Closter and Haworth
Northern Valley Old Tappan High School:  Old Tappan, Harrington Park, Norwood and Northvale.


Resident Students
If you are new to the district or live in the district and did not attend a district school, please click on 1 or 2 for the town you live in or are moving to.
1) If you reside or are moving to Closter, Demarest or Haworth, click here.
2) If you reside or are moving to Old Tappan, Harrington Park, Norwood or Northvale, click here. 
Please note - If you live in the district and are attending a district middle school (Haworth Middle School, Demarest Middle School, Tenakill Middle School, Harrington Park School, Nathan Hale School, Norwood P School), you may register to the appropriate high school in March of the year your student is in 8th grade.  You will only need to complete Part 3 of the registration which is the Genesis Online Registration.
















Non-Resident Tuition Students


Tuition cost for 2026-2027 school year has not yet been determined. 


If you are interested in attending either high school but do not live in the district, please complete the application for Non-Resident Tuition Students for the school you are interested in applying to by clicking on the link below. Also, follow the instructions regarding the documents that you must provide and be sure to email the completed application to all email addresses on the application. Only completed applications will be reviewed.  
After reviewing your completed application and required documents, the Administration will forward their recommendation to the Superintendent for final decision. You will be contacted as soon as the Superintendent makes a decision.  If approved, you will be notified of the next steps you must take to enroll your child. 
If you are interested in attending NVHS at Demarest  NVD Non-Resident Tuition Application 
If you are interested in attending NVHS at Old Tappan  NVOT Non-Resident Tuition Application
