Once the application is submitted a confirmation page will open. On the top of the page you will find directions to schedule a virtual meeting to finalize the enrollment. The enrollment
IS NOT
complete until you have this meeting. Be sure to select the correct Service Type based on your child’s grade for 2026-2027. (TK/K, 1-5, 6-12).
Incorrect appointment types will be canceled and you will need to schedule the correct appointment to finalize the enrollment.
The first available appointment is on December 8 - open for TK & K ONLY
January 12 - enrollment appointments open for all grades.
1. Requirements for Enrollment
Student must be living full-time in Fremont, CA at the time of enrollment.
A student cannot be enrolled in an FUSD school for the current school year if they are enrolled in another public or private school for the current school year.
Click here to view current vaccine / immunization requirements
.
2. Student Placement in a School and Grade Level
School Locator
(woolpert.com)
to find a home school (boundaries) by residence address. Students may be placed outside their home school based on availability.
Elementary schools participate in a
kindergarten lottery/wait lists
when more kindergarten students are enrolled than available space.
Click here to access the Overload Complaint Form
.
Student must be 5 years of age on or before September 1st of that school year to enroll in kindergarten.
Kindergarten is extended day format.
Click here to learn more about Transitional Kindergarten
, including age requirements and benefits for students.
If your child is too young for our TK or kindergarten program, please check out our
Preschool Program
.
Initial grade placement is based on chronological age. (
Board Policy
)

3. Special Programs
PLEASE NOTE:
To be considered for a special program you must ENROLL in FUSD, as indicated on this page, as well as submit the program application.
Students interested in a specialized program who live outside of Fremont must apply to the program of interest
first.
Once offered a seat, you will go to your district of residence and apply for an inter-district transfer.
