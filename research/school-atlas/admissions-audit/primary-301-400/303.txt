Find Your School
Students in the Bellevue School District are assigned to schools based on the location of their residence; the assigned school is referred to as attendance area or neighborhood school. Resident students are guaranteed enrollment at their attendance area school.
Bellevue School District's School Locator
Find your attendance area school
How to use the Bellevue School District's School Locator
Find your attendance area school
The Bellevue School District's School Locator tool will help families find their neighborhood elementary, middle, and high schools.
Visit the
Bellevue School District's School Locator
For information on bus routes, visit the
Bus Routes Information
webpage.
How to use the Bellevue School District's School Locator
Step1
Visit:
Bellevue School District's School Locator
Step 2
Enter an address in the search bar to find your neighborhood elementary, middle, and high schools.
Map Navigation: If you don't know the address, or it cannot be found, pan and zoom in the map to find your area of interest and click within the map to identify the schools and director district associated with that location.
Results Panel: A marker will appear on the map indicating the address or selected location. If the address or selected location is within the school district, the panel on the left side will populate with results, indicating the director district and elementary, middle, and high schools.
Step 3
Click on the results to highlight the associated attendance area within the map. Click on the magnifying glass button for any of the results （bottom right corner） to zoom to the extent of the chosen attendance area.
Map Widgets: Use the basemap and zoom in/out widgets in the top-left corner of the map pane to change the background map or zoom in/out. Use the widgets in the top-right corner of the map pane to return to the full extent of the district, use your mobile device’s location, access the map layer list, or view the map legend.
Step 4
If families would like to print the result, use the print button at the top of the results pane to download a PDF listing the schools and director district associated with the address or selected location.
Step 5
Click “Clear search location” within the top-center of the map pane or the “X” on the right side of the Search bar to clear the search.
iFrames are not supported on this page.

We acknowledge that we are on the indigenous lands of the Coast Salish, including the Duwamish, Muckleshoot, and Snoqualmie peoples.
We thank these caretakers of this land who have lived and continue to live here since time immemorial. We reflect on the historic context that brings us to reside on this land and accept responsibility to interrupt erasure. We are committed to our ongoing partnership with the Snoqualmie Tribe as we listen to, learn from, and lead with their guidance.
