Squares and the map
Counts of exact national ranks in this edition. Schools U.S. News ranks only as a range are counted apart, never as the range’s top.
Who moved
Top 100 only, and only when U.S. News printed both ranks and they agree with the school’s other years.
The long view
Only ranks U.S. News printed for the school on its list or the school’s own page, from 2019 on. A gap means no printed rank on file for that edition; a line stops where U.S. News changed how it ranked the state, or where the school itself changed.
Typical school
Medians of schools ranked in this edition that report the figure. Test scores are not compared across states: each state sets its own test.
