BRITISH INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL OF WASHINGTON

TUITION FEES

The British International School of Washington offers DC's finest education for Early Years, Primary, and Secondary students. Read on to learn about our private school tuition fees and what they cover.
  2. Admissions
  3. Tuition Fees

Your child will love coming into school every day, inspired by world-class teachers, exclusive global collaborations, and a breadth of extracurricular activities. Our approach shapes generations of confident, creative, and resilient global citizens who are set for lifelong success. 

If you have any questions about the fees we charge at our international school in Washington, please get in touch with our Admissions Department. 

 

The British International School of Washington's tuition and fees ensure that BISW students enjoy DC's finest international education in engaging, rigorous learning environments. Our students love coming to school and receiving the tools they need for a bright future.

What do BISW tuition fees cover?


Teaching

  • Classroom instruction, supervision, and in-school workshops

Meal Plan

  • Pre-Nursery through Year 11 enrollment packages include the Meal Plan.

  • Enrollment packages for Years 12 and 13 do not include the Meal Plan, BUT parents and guardians can opt-in their child. Contact the Finance Department for details.

  • Lunches include an entree, two sides, a dessert, and a drink. Learn more here.

Assessment Costs

  • IB Diploma examinations, IGCSEs and Cognitive Ability Tests.

Supplies and Resources

  • Exercise books; the loan of textbooks, supplies, educational materials, and documentation; 1-1 access to iPads for Years 3-6 in-school and 1-1 tablet or laptop access in-school for Years 7-13; subscriptions and educational memberships; and yearbook

Trips

  • Whole-year local field trips (Years 7-11) and an environmental service trip (Year 12); single event/day field trips, additional activities, and school-run after-school activities


2026-27 Registration and Enrollment Fees

 

Application Fee — $190

  • This is a non-refundable fee.

Enrollment Deposit — $2,000

  • This deposit is due each year to secure enrollment or re-enrollment.

  • It becomes an advance tuition payment subtracted from a later tuition payment.

New Enrollment Fee — $2,700

  • This one-time fee contributes to important capital enhancements at the British International School of Washington.

  • It is non-refundable and in addition to the tuition schedule

  • This fee is due only before the first year of enrollment only.

Refundable Security Deposit — $1,000

  • This fee covers any loss or damage

  • It is due the first year of enrollment to the secondary school

 

Additional Fees

 

Potential Obligated Supplementary Fees

  • Learning Support, exceeding 3 hours per week of provision

Required Supplementary Costs

  • School uniform

Optional Supplementary Costs

  • Additional overnight/multi-day field trips (e.g., MIT visit, Juilliard camps, Global Games, Primary Camps, World Scholars Cup, ski trip, or overnight sports tournaments), on a trip-by-trip basis

  • IB and IGCSE retakes/remarks, SAT, SAT II Subject Tests, ACT, or other non-compulsory assessments

  • School bus transportation (Contact the Transportation Department for details.)

  • Childcare (after-school and vacation programs)

  • Externally run clubs, individual music lessons, other language courses, or individual private tutoring

  • School portraits (annual individual and class photos, including graduation photos for Year 13 students)
