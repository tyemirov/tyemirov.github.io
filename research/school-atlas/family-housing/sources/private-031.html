Tuition and Financial Aid

Since our founding, Commonwealth School has been dedicated to increasing access and welcoming as many qualified students as possible through extensive need-based financial aid. Making tuition more affordable and within budget for our families continues to be an institutional priority. This empowers us to attract strong applicants from a wide range of backgrounds and to compose a well-rounded and talented student body.

Financial Aid Grants and More

We remain committed to partnering with families to meet the cost of a Commonwealth education. Commonwealth’s financial aid includes textbooks, biannual all-school trips, and participation in clubs and sports. We also offer supplemental grants for transportation and technology; additional scholarships for summer programs and school-sponsored international trips; discounted standardized testing, test preparation, and tutoring; and flexible payment plans.
 
Applying for financial aid at Commonwealth takes less than thirty minutes. 

Flexible Payment Plans

All Commonwealth families have three payment plan options; they select one annually that works best for their budget:

  • Ten Monthly Payments (from May through February)
  • Two Payments (by June 1 and October 1)
  • One Payment (by June 1)

Families of returning students should consult the Parent Portal (login required) for information that is specific to them.

Access and Affordability

All students can avail themselves of all opportunities at Commonwealth through additional grants for technology, transportation, and international travel, as well as sliding-scale costs for tutoring, standardized tests, and more.

  • Technology grants are made available to eligible families and can be applied to purchasing a MacBook Pro laptop, printer, and software through Commonwealth and at a reduced cost. There is no cost to the families of students with a financial aid percentage of 90 or higher. For the 2026–2027 school year, on average, families paid $185 for this package.
  • Transportation stipends offset some or all of the cost of commuting to Commonwealth via the MBTA.
  • Chatfield Cultural Scholarships help cover student travel and exchanges, as well as travel-related independent projects.
  • Discounted professional tutoring, standardized tests, and test preparation are also available to students with financial aid grants.
