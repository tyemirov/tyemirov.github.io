Tuition

Tuition reflects small classes, expert faculty, and an individualized approach for intellectually curious students with growth mindsets.
Sibling Discount
We believe in family and support keeping siblings together. We offer a sibling discount for families with more than one student at the school. ​Tuition will be reduced by:
  • 10% for the second (younger) child
  • 20% for third and additional younger children enrolled
​

2026-2027 tuition:
​1st - 3rd Grades:     $26,750  
4th - 8th Grades:   $30,750
​9th - 12th Grades:  $32,750
​

Tuition FAQs:

What does tuition include?
  • ​Small classes
  • Expert faculty
  • Hands-on activities
  • Engineering
  • Computer Science
  • After-school activity period from 4:15-5:15pm
  • Field trips
  • Technology (Windows laptops for Upper Elementary to Upper School). Students may also bring their own device.
 
How often do you raise tuition? ​
  • ​We strive to keep tuition increases modest and predictable for our families. For historical reference, the tuition when we opened in the 2015-2016 academic year was $24,500 for 4th-7th grade.
​
Do you offer financial aid?
  • ​We are evaluating implementing a needs-based financial aid program for future years to help families.
​
​Do you have payment plans or fees?
  • Yes, parents may pay in one lump sum, 2 equal payments, or 10 installments.
  • There is a $250 fee for the 2-payment plan and $500 for the 10-payment plan.
