    From Veritas Collegiate Academy

    Veritas Collegiate is pleased to announce the launch of online courses. Our institution is a multi-campus global Classical Christian school with locations in Hampton Roads and Arlington, Virginia; Beijing, Shenzhen, Shanghai, and Kunshan, China; and Budapest, Hungary.

    Founded in 2004 by Sean and Maria Elgut, we are committed to developing future leaders. Our rigorous training fosters clear thinking, careful listening, and critical discernment, equipping students to articulate their ideas precisely. Graduates are prepared to evaluate life experiences through the lens of Scripture with enthusiasm.

    We proudly report that our average SAT score for the class of 2024 is 1410, one of the highest in our region. Our alumni regularly gain admission to prestigious universities, including Cornell University, UCLA, UNC Chapel Hill, USC, Vanderbilt University, Johns Hopkins University, Georgetown University, Rice University, and others.

    As a graduate of Veritas Collegiate Academy, having attended from 4th through 12th grade, I can confidently say this school shaped me in the most meaningful ways.
