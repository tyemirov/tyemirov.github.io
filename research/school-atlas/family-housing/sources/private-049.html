FAQs

We know many of our prospective families have questions before they apply. Read below for answers to some of our most frequently asked questions, and know you can always reach out to the Admissions Office for more information. 

Application and Financial Aid FAQs

  • When is the application deadline?
  • What is the application fee
  • What is Marlborough’s tuition?
  • Will there be any additional costs?
  • Does Marlborough offer payment plans?
  • Does Marlborough offer financial aid?
  • Do I apply for financial aid before or after I apply for admission to Marlborough?
  • Should I apply for financial aid or not?
  • What expenses are covered by financial aid at Marlborough School?

When is the application deadline?

The application for the 2027-2028 school year is now available. Our deadline is on January 8, 2027. If you have any questions, please contact admissions@marlborough.org.

What is the application fee

The application fee is $150. We do offer fee waivers if this presents a financial hardship, contact admissions for the waiver.

What is Marlborough’s tuition?

For the 2026-2027 school year, Marlborough’s tuition is $57,850. There is also a New Student Fee of $3,300.

Will there be any additional costs?

Yes. Additional expenses include books, class trips, uniforms, transportation (bus service if warranted), and extracurricular fees, among others.

Does Marlborough offer payment plans?

Yes. Tuition can be paid in one payment, two payments, or nine payments. 

Does Marlborough offer financial aid?

Marlborough has a very generous financial aid program. Awards range from 5% to 100% of School costs and it is based on financial need.

Do I apply for financial aid before or after I apply for admission to Marlborough?

The process takes place concurrently, though there are separate deadlines and supporting documentation for each aspect of the process.

Should I apply for financial aid or not?

If you believe you may need assistance in covering the cost of tuition at any point during your daughter’s attendance at Marlborough, we recommend applying for financial aid. Once a student is enrolled at Marlborough who is not receiving financial aid (because they never initially applied during the application process), assistance will only be available to a student should your family experience a significant change in financial circumstances.

What expenses are covered by financial aid at Marlborough School?

All expenses that are part of a Marlborough School experience are covered by financial aid. This includes books, technology, outdoor education fees, the college tour, transportation, and some fees associated with extracurricular activities.

Admission FAQs

  • How many applications do you receive each year?
  • Do you accept transfer students?
  • Does Marlborough accept international students?
  • Who will you consider applications from?

How many applications do you receive each year?

The number of applications we receive from year to year varies, but it is important to note that it is a competitive applicant pool with limited spaces per grade level. 

Do you accept transfer students?

We do not admit students mid-year in non-entry grades (entry grades are Grades 7 and 9). We do admit girls into non-entry grades, but spaces are extremely limited for Grades 8, 10, and 11. We do not admit Seniors.

Does Marlborough accept international students?

Marlborough does not issue the I-20 form for foreign student study and only enrolls students who intend to complete Marlborough through the 12th Grade. Students must live in Los Angeles, CA with at least one of their parents or a legal guardian while attending Marlborough. We do not offer a program in English as a Second Language (ESL), so all students must be completely fluent in English for reading, speaking, and composition.

Who will you consider applications from?

Aligned with our mission and long-standing history, Marlborough maintains its identity as a girls' school, prioritizing the unique needs, shared experiences, and voices of young women. Marlborough will consider for admission any applicant who identifies as female.

General Marlborough FAQs

  • What is the daily schedule at Marlborough?
  • I don’t want my daughter to get “lost” in the crowd. Do the girls get personalized attention?
  • How does Marlborough help students adjust to a new school?
  • Do you have a library on campus?
  • What is the uniform at Marlborough?
  • Does Marlborough have any religious affiliation?

What is the daily schedule at Marlborough?

We have a rotating, block schedule that begins at 8:30 a.m. and ends at 3:00 p.m. each day. The day is broken into four class blocks as well as community times.

I don’t want my daughter to get “lost” in the crowd. Do the girls get personalized attention?

There are many adults who watch over the girls. In addition to her teachers, each student is placed in a grade-level Advisory group, has a grade-level Dean, and a teaching team that works with the entire class. There is a Head of Middle School (Grades 7-9), Upper School (Grades 10-12), and a Director of Studies who oversee the curricular program, and a Dean of Student Life who oversees the extracurricular program.

How does Marlborough help students adjust to a new school?

Marlborough students are invited to a New Family Celebration in the spring before they enroll. New students also participate in “Violets 101” which introduces the girls to each other and to the School.  

Do you have a library on campus?

Our Shari and Edward Glazer Center for Entrepreneurship and Innovation (CEI) houses our library, but it is so much more! The CEI serves as a space for students to connect, collaborate, and create.

What is the uniform at Marlborough?

Middle School students wear gray skirts, Upper School students wear black skirts.  Students may also wear black or gray pants or shorts. White, black, or purple polos; white, black, or gray sweaters, jackets, or sweatshirts; and closed-toed shoes are also required to be in uniform.

Does Marlborough have any religious affiliation?

Marlborough is not affiliated with any religious organization.

Marlborough is a college preparatory,  private middle and upper school for girls located in the heart of Los Angeles. Our independent school prides itself on inspiring middle and high school-aged students to discover who they are, engage deeply in their studies, embrace their communities and lead lives of purpose.
