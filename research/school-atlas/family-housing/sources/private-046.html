The Value of a Quarry Lane Education

We understand that school tuition is a substantial investment for families and we think it's important for our families to know what their investment goes towards. Quarry Lane's tuition revenue helps us in our commitment to:

  • Attracting, maintaining and developing the highest quality faculty
  • Offering the depth and breadth of programs and curriculum available at Quarry Lane
  • Maintaining our top-notch facilities and resources
