          Access & Affordability

          Our financial aid program reduces the cost of tuition for eligible families, supports our commitment to socio-economic diversity, and makes Menlo a reality for any admitted student.

          We believe funding an independent school education should be a high priority for all Menlo families. Menlo’s tuition at all levels supports our programs and our people, while our financial aid program is funded by generous donations from our community.

          This year, 22% of Menlo families received financial assistance towards tuition and non-tuition expenses such as books, athletic team or club trips, global trips, a laptop, athletic gear, and more.

          With a $8.6 million financial aid budget, we meet 100% of each admitted student’s demonstrated financial need. Tuition for families receiving grants was reduced by amounts ranging from 11% to 99% for the 2026-2027 school year.

          It’s important that you know that the admissions and financial aid processes at Menlo are entirely separate. Applying for financial aid will not affect your child’s admissions decision.

          We are dedicated to making a Menlo education accessible to admitted students, regardless of their financial circumstances. This program enables us to create a diverse and inclusive learning environment and enrich the educational experience for all students.

          Tuition & What’s Included

          Full tuition and fees for the 2026-2027 school year are $67,792 and include:

          • Lunch
          • Menlo Bus Transportation
          • Shuttle to/from Menlo Park Caltrain
          • Most field trips and retreats
          • Athletic and arts program participation

          Non-tuition expenses 

          • Books/supplies approx. $500-$700*
          • Laptop approx. $1,300*

          *Financial Aid also supports families with tuition and non-tuition expenses.

          Clarity Financial Aid Application

          Menlo uses Clarity, a third-party application system, to determine your financial need based on the information you enter. There, you will also authorize Clarity to directly download and verify your tax returns from the IRS. The process can take as little as 30 minutes to complete.

          To begin, create an account on Clarity. There is a $65 application fee, which covers your Menlo application and your application to any other school using Clarity. Clarity automatically waives the fee for applicants who meet its income-level guidelines. If you need but don’t receive an automatic waiver through Clarity, please contact the Financial Aid Office, financialaid@menloschool.org

          Will I qualify for financial aid?

          Menlo offers financial assistance based on each family’s unique financial situation. When evaluating need, we consider many factors, including but not limited to:

          • Family income
          • Household size
          • Number of children attending tuition-based schools
          • Family net worth
          • Extraordinary expenses

          Each family’s circumstances are evaluated individually, and calculator results can not guarantee your eligibility or level of assistance.

          Financial aid data updated for the 25-26 school year.

          Financial Aid Questions

          Approximately 23% of the student body receives financial aid. Based on individual family circumstances, families from a wide range of income levels have qualified for assistance, and Menlo School has been able to meet 100% of the demonstrated need for each student it enrolls. This year, tuition for families receiving grants was reduced by amounts ranging from 11% to 99% for the 2026-2027school year.

          The financial aid application process runs concurrently with the admission application process. Financial aid applications and tax documents for income verification are submitted through the Clarity platform and are due on January 5, 2027, for families applying to Menlo for Fall 2027.

          Prospective families’ financial aid applications are due on January 5, 2027, the same date as the admission applications. Financial aid grant information is provided simultaneously with the admission decision so that families have all the information they need to decide on enrollment. Current families returning to Menlo for Fall 2027 must submit their applications and materials for current students by November 1, 2026.

          Families must apply by the posted deadlines to be eligible for aid for the following academic year. Menlo maintains funding to assist current families who experience verified financial reversals outside the regular application cycle, allowing them to apply for financial aid for the first time.

          No, not at all. The Admissions Committee considers each applicant without knowing whether or not the applicant’s family is applying for financial aid. Once students are admitted, the Financial Aid Committee meets to review the financial aid applications of admitted students to determine the size of each student’s grant.

          Regardless of custodial arrangements, all legal parents/guardians must complete a financial aid application and submit all pertinent tax documents (1040s, W-2s, and any relevant tax schedules).

          Yes. We have three different payment options. All newly enrolling families pay a non-refundable enrollment deposit by noon on March 19, 2027 and choose one of the three payment plans:

          One (1) Payment – This plan is for families who would like to make one payment for the full tuition on June 1.

          Two (2) Payments — This plan requires families to pay 70% of tuition by June 1 and the remaining balance by September 1.

          Ten (10) Payments — Menlo offers a ten-month payment plan administered by the Business Office. This budget plan divides the total yearly tuition (less the 10% deposit) into ten equal monthly installments paid by direct debit beginning June 1 and ending March 1. An interest charge of 4% of the remaining tuition balance is calculated monthly.

          We do not offer academic, athletic, or other merit scholarships. Financial aid at Menlo is completely need-based.

          Yes. We will ask you to file a new financial aid application and submit your tax declarations and W-2 and/or 1099 forms each year. The school will continue to grant aid appropriate for your demonstrated need until your child graduates.

          Admission and financial grant decisions for new students will be emailed on March 19. If accepted and granted financial aid, the grant amount will be included in the contract. 

          Financial aid grant decisions for returning students will be included with re-enrollment contracts on January 25, 2027.

          You should plan to complete your taxes by this deadline, but if that is not possible, you must provide an estimate of your 2026 income. Please contact the Financial Aid Office at financialaid@menloschool.org if you can’t meet this deadline.

          Yes! Menlo wants all students to be able to fully engage in our program. Financial aid grants go beyond the tuition discount and include the same percentage discount on all non-tuition school expenses, from books to laptops, athletic gear, school trips, AP exams, and more. Menlo will support families with all of these school expenses.

          If you have questions regarding your financial aid application, please contact our Financial Aid Office at financialaid@menloschool.org, tel: 650-330-2001 ext. 2611.
