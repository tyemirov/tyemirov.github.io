Affordability

Lake Forest Academy seeks to make our education accessible to all qualified students.  

Lake Forest Academy offers over $6.3 million in financial aid grants annually. Financial aid grants are primarily need-based and vary in range depending upon each family’s financial situation. Additionally, we offer a limited number of merit scholarships, ranging from $10,000 - $15,000, which do not require an additional application. The Academy does not offer athletic scholarships nor other non-need based extracurricular scholarships.

Lake Forest Academy now partners with Clarity to process financial aid applications. Based on the financial information you provide, Clarity will generate an estimate of your family’s expected contribution to educational expenses. Our Financial Aid Committee will review Clarity’s recommendation along with your family’s individual circumstances to determine a final financial aid package.

Applying for Financial Aid

Complete the Clarity Application and submit supporting documents by the January 15, 2026 deadline.

Application

Please go to the Clarity Application and create an account. The application typically takes less than 30 minutes to complete. There is a $60 fee to submit, and you can share your application with other schools that accept the Clarity Application at no cost. 

In the case of divorced, separated or never married parents, both biological parents (and step-parents, if applicable) are required to submit a Clarity financial aid application.
 

Supporting Documents

Make sure to return to your dashboard after you complete and submit your Clarity application to upload additional tax documentation.

Clarity Support

 In-app support is available through the “Help” button at the bottom left of the screen, as well as email and phone support at support@clarityapp.com or 206-210-3752, in both English and Spanish.
