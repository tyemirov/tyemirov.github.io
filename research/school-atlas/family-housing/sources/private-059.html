Tuition and Tuition Assistance

2026-2027 TUITION

 

Pre-K/Kindergarten: $24,180

First to Fifth Grade: $28,220

Sixth to Eighth Grade: $33,370

Ninth to Twelfth Grade: $37,040

 

Tuition is payable in eight installments. Payments do not cover the cost of uniforms, books, supplies, lunches, laptops (if applicable), transportation, special event admission fees, or other expenses incurred in the ordinary course of a student’s activities at Berkeley.

TUITION ASSISTANCE

 

Berkeley Preparatory School is committed to enrolling a diverse and talented student body. We strive to make the educational experience accessible and affordable to all families regardless of their socioeconomic status. Berkeley is fortunate to have much of its tuition assistance budget funded through the generosity of donors who have established named endowments in support of the tuition assistance program.

 

A Note About Vouchers

 

Berkeley Preparatory School does not accept any money from any governmental entity for any reason. To do so would potentially leave the school vulnerable to governmental interference, jeopardizing the independence we cherish. We encourage families with demonstrated financial need to apply for tuition assistance, which is funded here at Berkeley in a variety of ways, though not through governmental subsidy.

Tuition Assistance Procedures

Tuition Assistance Overview

In determining need, Berkeley Preparatory School follows NAIS Principles of Good Practice. Tuition assistance awards are based upon FACTS Grant and Aid Assessment (FACTS) financial assessment of each applicant. It is important to note that Berkeley may not be able to accommodate all financial aid applicants in a given year. Complete applications and required documents received in the Admissions Office by the deadline will receive first priority relative to assistance dollars available. 

 

  • Deadline for Current Berkeley Students for the 2027-2028 School Year: December 15, 2026*

    • Award notification in late January

  • Deadline for New Families Applying to Berkeley for the 2027-2028 School Year: February 1, 2027*
    • Award notification at the end of February / early March 

 

*Tuition assistance applications received after the deadlines will be considered for our tuition assistance waiting pool.

 

All families applying for tuition assistance for the first time should contact Julia Loncar, Associate Director of Admissions and Tuition Assistance, by calling (813) 885-1673 or emailing loncajul@berkeleyprep.org. Families currently receiving assistance must submit a new tuition assistance application each year. If the financial circumstances remain the same, the school will make every effort to support a family at the same level of assistance.

How to Apply for Tuition Assistance

To apply for tuition assistance for the 2027-2028 school year, please complete the following steps no later than December 15, 2026* for returning Berkeley families, or February 1, 2027* for new applicants:

 

  1. Create your FACTS online account. 
  2. Complete your Tuition Assistance application.
  3. Submit your additional required documents listed below:
    • Upload your 2024 and 2025 1040 income tax return(s) with signatures
    • Business/Farm Statement and Corporate Tax Return/Schedule C/Schedule F (if applicable)
    • Upload your 2024 and 2025 W-2 Forms (if applicable)
    • Upload a copy of your most recent pay stub(s)
    • By April 15, 2027, upload a copy of your 2026 federal tax forms with all supporting schedules and W-2 Forms (if applicable)

 

*Tuition assistance applications received after the deadlines will be considered for our tuition assistance waiting pool.

 

Note to families using non-U.S. dollars and tax structure: Due to the difficulty in evaluating need using non-U.S. dollars and tax structure, a family must provide 2024 and 2025 U.S. tax returns in order to apply for tuition assistance.

Divorced, Separated, or Never-Married Parent/Guardian

Recognizing that each parent bears a responsibility for financing a student’s education, financial information from all family members is required. Custodial and non-custodial parents (regardless of legal settlements, who are divorced, separated, or never married) must submit their own tuition assistance application. The Tuition Assistance Committee will consider all household assets in determining need.

Tuition Assistance Deadlines

October 1: Tuition Assistance Applications Available Online

 

December 15: Tuition Assistance Applications Due (Current Berkeley Students)

 

February 1: Tuition Assistance Applications Due (New Families applying to Berkeley)

 

End of February / Early March: Notification of Tuition Assistance Decisions for accepted students

Tuition Assistance FAQ

What is tuition assistance?

Tuition assistance is an award based on a family’s demonstrated need, as determined by FACTS calculations, tax returns, and Berkeley’s Tuition Assistance Committee. Tuition assistance is an award, not a loan, and therefore, is not paid back to the school.

What is “demonstrated need”?

“Demonstrated need” is defined as the difference between the annual tuition and the amount a family can be expected to contribute to the cost of the student’s education. The tuition assistance application and tax returns are used to determine need.

Does Berkeley offer merit scholarships?

Berkeley’s tuition assistance is need-based. We do not offer academic, athletic, or artistic merit-based scholarships.

When will I receive a decision regarding tuition assistance?

Tuition assistance will be awarded at the end of February / early March after acceptance and prior to the deposit deadline.

Do I have to re-apply for tuition assistance each year?

Yes. Since a family’s financial circumstances are subject to change, we require reapplication for tuition assistance each year. If your family’s financial situation remains stable, you can expect, relatively, the same percentage of tuition assistance, understanding tuition and fees are likely to increase each year.

Who can apply for tuition assistance?

Anyone may apply for tuition assistance. We recognize that each family’s financial situation is unique.
