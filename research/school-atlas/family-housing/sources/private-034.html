Affording LWHS

The Flexible Tuition program helps ensure LWHS can fulfill its mission of developing the head, heart, and hands of highly motivated students from all walks of life. Providing access and maintaining socioeconomic diversity at LWHS is paramount and crucial to a healthy and dynamic learning environment.

Lick-Wilmerding uses the Clarity Application to determine a family’s level of financial need based on the information that families share in the application.

To get started, please go to the Clarity Application and create an account. The application typically takes 30 minutes to complete. The Clarity Application is also mobile-friendly so you can complete it from anywhere. You are also able to save your progress and return at any time.

While there is a fee to complete the application, fee waivers are automatically granted to qualifying families. If you are in need of a fee waiver but did not receive one, please reach out to us at admission@lwhs.org. 

If you need support during the process of completing your application, there is support available through the “Help” button on the Clarity Application, as well as email and phone support in both English and Spanish at support@clarityapp.com or (206) 210-3752. The application itself is also fully translated into Spanish. 

Flexible Tuition Application Process

Flexible Tuition enrollment contracts for newly enrolling families are due Thursday, March 18, 2027 by Noon.

  • Create a Clarity Tuition Account
  • Prepare 2025 & 2026 Tax Returns
  • Submit the Flexible Tuition Application
  • Upload 2025 tax information and LWHS Supplemental Information
  • Notification & Enrollment

Create a Clarity Tuition Account

To be eligible to participate in the Flexible Tuition program at LWHS, please create a parent/guardian account with Clarity Tuition.

Note: Parents/Guardians living in separate households must submit separate applications.

Prepare 2025 & 2026 Tax Returns

We understand that many families may not have their 2026 tax returns ready by the March 1, 2027 deadline. We recommend making an appointment with your tax preparer (if you use one) and/or asking your employer if early W-2 and K-1 forms are available. If your 2026 forms are not ready, you may submit estimated figures.

Submit the Flexible Tuition Application

Complete and submit your Flexible Tuition application through Clarity by January 5, 2027.

  • The submission fee is $60, and fee waivers are automatically granted to qualifying families.
  •  The LWHS Clarity application will be available starting in November.

Note: Parents/guardians living in separate households must each submit a separate application.

Upload 2025 tax information and LWHS Supplemental Information

Submit 2026 IRS Tax form 1040, W-2, and all schedules to your Clarity Tuition parent/guardian account. 

Notification & Enrollment

Admission decisions and Flexible Tuition awards will be mailed on Thursday, March 11, 2027, and will be available online in Ravenna on Friday, March 12, 2027.

Families enrolling with Flexible Tuition, enrollment contracts are due by Thursday, March 18, 2027 at 12:00 PM.

Income is only one of the factors that goes into determining Flexible Tuition. Each student’s Flexible Tuition is determined on a case-by-case basis because every family is unique. For more information about how Flexible Tuition is calculated, please click here.

School Tuition

2026–2027 School Year: $64,900 (for returning students) and $65,900 (for new students; inclusive of $1,000 new student fee)

Payment Plans

LWHS offers three payment plan options to families: 

  1. Full Payment - Full payment option
  2. Standard Payment Plan - Three payment option (+$50)
  3. 10 Monthly Payments - Ten payment option (+$50)

Thanks to its 130 year history of being accessible to students from all walks of life, including 77 years of being tuition free, Lick-Wilmerding High School enjoys the legacy, the resources, and the strategic resolve to push the limits on what it can mean to be a truly accessible independent school. The definition of Flexible Tuition is: the amount of tuition paid by LWHS families who participate in the Flexible Tuition Program.
