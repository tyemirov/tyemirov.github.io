Affording Head-Royce School

Choosing a school is an investment in your child’s future.
Explore Head-Royce tuition, need-based financial assistance,
and resources to help you plan.

 
 

Access to a Head‑Royce Education

Socioeconomic diversity is an important part of the Head‑Royce community. Different perspectives and experiences deepen conversations, broaden understanding, and enrich learning for every student.

We offer need-based tuition assistance to make a Head‑Royce education accessible to families across a wide range of financial circumstances. More than a quarter of our students receive tuition assistance, with more than $8 million awarded each year.

We encourage every family to explore what's possible.

Tuition covers most school-related costs, with the exception of textbooks, supplies, global education travel, food, and after-school care. Families may pay tuition in one payment, two payments, or through a nine-month payment plan.

Could Tuition Assistance Be Right for Your Family?

Families come to Head‑Royce with a wide range of financial circumstances. If the full cost of tuition would place a strain on your family, we encourage you to explore tuition assistance and consider applying. You do not need to determine on your own whether you will qualify before starting the process.

Tuition Assistance at Head‑Royce

Tuition assistance is need-based and awarded as a grant that does not need to be repaid. Awards are based on each family’s demonstrated financial need and the availability of funds. Applying for tuition assistance has no bearing on admission decisions; the two processes are entirely separate.

Head‑Royce uses Clarity to manage tuition assistance applications. The online application takes less than 30 minutes to complete, is available in English and Spanish, and is mobile-friendly. New families will create a Clarity account to begin the application; returning families can use their existing credentials.

Tuition assistance applications are due January 4, 2027 for new families.

See all deadlines

What You'll Need to Apply

  • Most recent paystub (hourly or salaried employees)
  • Schedule K-1, Form 1120S, and/or Form 1065 (S-corporation shareholders or partnership partners)
  • Form 1040 with all schedules (Clarity pulls this directly from the IRS)

Each household must submit a separate application, including in cases of separated, divorced, or non-custodial parents, regardless of financial arrangements between households.

Tutition FAQs

No. Admissions and financial aid decisions are made independently of one another. Head-Royce admits students without regard to financial circumstances, though families should be aware that our assistance budget, while robust, is not unlimited.

Clarity considers income, assets, family size, age of parents, and the number of children in tuition-charging institutions. It then calculates a suggested family contribution based on discretionary income — the more discretionary income a family has, the higher their suggested contribution.

Head-Royce requires both parents to contribute to the cost of education to the extent of their ability. A completed application is required from each household. All information is kept strictly confidential.

Families must reapply each year. Once assistance has been awarded, it is reasonable to expect renewal provided your financial situation has not changed substantially and your student remains in good standing.
