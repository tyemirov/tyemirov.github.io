​​We recognize that a Milton Academy education is a significant investment in your child’s future.

Our focus is on creating a unique, valuable experience that celebrates each student’s individuality–and our diversity is a hallmark of the school. Every year, we actively seek young people with academic promise and a range of talents, skills and interests from all socio-economic backgrounds.

2026-2027 Tuition

Milton charges a single, annual tuition that includes on-campus student experiences as well as tuition insurance; the school does not charge mandatory fees.  

Classes I-IV (boarding): $79,900

Classes I-IV (day): $69,900

Boarding student tuition includes all of the above as well as room & board and health center services. 

Upper School day students are served lunch and also are welcome to join boarders for weekday dinners as well as on and off campus weekend activities, including meals. 

Please note that book and supply charges will be added separately to students’ incidental accounts (IAs) based on student class selections. 

All students are expected to bring a laptop computer to school.

Optional Programs*

Families also may choose to enroll their students in other, optional programs at Milton including:

  • Daily bus service to and from school 
  • Music lessons  
  • Cultural exchanges 
  • Pre-season athletic trips 

*Additional fees will be charged

Opportunities For FINANCIAL AID

We are deeply committed to creating an environment that is open and accessible to outstanding and qualified applicants, regardless of their families’ financial circumstances. We strive to meet 100% of the demonstrated need for each enrolled student; Milton does not discriminate on the basis of any category protected by federal, state, or local law, including but not limited to gender, race, color, disability, sexual orientation, religion, or national or ethnic origin.

Applying for Financial Aid

Families applying for financial assistance must submit a financial aid application before January 31st. To get started, please go to the Financial Aid Application and create an account. The application typically takes less than 30 minutes to complete and is mobile-friendly so you can complete it from anywhere. The submission fee is $65.

Families often find Clarity instructions helpful in completing the financial aid application; there is in-app support available as well as email support at support@claritytuition.com or 206-210-3752 in both English and Spanish.

Financial aid information is included with all Milton admission packages. Awards for accepted students will be communicated along with the admission decision on March 10th.

Determining Need For Financial Aid

Milton Academy looks at many factors in assessing “need,” which Milton defines as the difference between the total cost of attendance (tuition, books, etc.) and a family’s ability to pay that cost. The need assessment is based upon income and family expenses, assets and liabilities, family size, cost of living and the number of children for whom a family must pay tuition. 

Milton expects that all parents who seek aid are employed, unless they are caretakers for children under the age of five, provide live-in care for an elderly dependent, or have a prohibitive disability. In the case of divorced or separated parents, both parents are required to complete financial aid applications, and the income and assets of both parents (and new spouses, if any) are taken into consideration. Although families must reapply for financial aid each year, a family should expect that grants would be renewed at the original level of funding unless there is a significant change in demonstrated need.

It is our sincere hope that families are not discouraged by the costs of independent education or by the process of applying for financial aid. If you are uncertain about your eligibility or have questions about the process, we encourage you to contact the Office of Financial Aid at 617-898-2239.

Financial Aid Application Timeline for New Candidates

January 31: Final date for parents/guardians (and any other adult living in the applicant’s primary residence) to complete and submit the financial aid application.

March 10: Milton sends preliminary financial aid assessment with the offer of admission.

International Applicants

Financial aid is available to applicants who are not American citizens or legal permanent residents of the United States. To be considered for financial aid, you must submit the financial aid application. In addition, you should submit 2024 and 2025 tax documents, including a translation into English where appropriate. You must also submit:

  • a letter from your employer specifying the terms of your employment
  • a certificate of finance from your bank(s) stating all bank account balances
  • a copy of your November and December 2026 bank statements.

Current Students Applying–or Reapplying–for Financial Aid

Milton will review your aid application each year; you must reapply by December 15. If there is no significant change in your family’s financial situation, you can expect a comparable aid assessment from year to year. Milton will not review aid applications for the upcoming year prior to the full payment of your tuition and fees for the current academic year. Financial aid awards will be communicated along with your enrollment contract in early spring.

Students applying for aid for the first time:

You must apply by December 15 and demonstrate that your financial circumstances have changed significantly since your initial enrollment. A cover letter explaining your change in circumstances must be submitted to the Director of Financial Aid by this deadline as well.
