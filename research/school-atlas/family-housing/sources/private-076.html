Affordability & Tuition

Making an Impact from the Hill and Beyond

Investing in a St. Stephen’s education means that together, with you, we are preparing your child to make an indelible and positive impact not only while they are students here but also in the world and beyond. While a St. Stephen’s education is unique, its access is open to all. Financial aid grants support our efforts to enroll a diverse and well-rounded student body from Austin, the United States and around the world.

Affordability 

In the 2026-27 school year, 22% of the student body received over $6.6 million in financial assistance, with awards ranging from $1,880 to more than $60,000. These awards aided families with annual incomes ranging from below $50,000 to over $200,000.

We offer on campus and virtual affordability workshops. Click here to register for the December 8 Virtual Affordability Workshop at 7 p.m. Topics include completing the Clarity financial assistance application, payment plans, merit boarding scholarships and answering general questions about the process. This is a parents-only session.

Signature Scholarship Programs

  • The Brewster Scholarships
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Scholarship
  • ISAS/SAES Scholars
  • Kavcic Family Scholars
  • Rural Texas Scholarships

The Brewster Scholarships

Awarded to curious, engaged and caring citizens who value the community above the individual and who garner a deep respect of both adults and their peers. The Brewster Scholarship covers the boarding supplement for each year the student is at St. Stephen’s. 

Martin Luther King Jr. Scholarship

A need-based award for one incoming boarding student who embodies the teachings of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. “The function of education is to teach one to think intensively and to think critically. Intelligence plus character — that is the goal of true education.” 

ISAS/SAES Scholars

Awarded annually to a student who is committed to a high set of values including integrity, character, inclusiveness, tolerance and service. Applicants must be currently enrolled in an Independent Schools Association of the Southwest (ISAS) or Southwestern Association of Episcopal Schools (SAES) accredited institution that does not offer a high school option. 

Kavcic Family Scholars

The Kavcic Family Scholarship funds four partial merit-based scholarships per year. The scholarships are awarded to students who demonstrate strong academic promise and will engage meaningfully in programs and community life beyond the classroom.

Rural Texas Scholarships

Established by the G.R. White Trust, the Rural Texas scholarship endowment provides support to boarding students who reside in rural Texas counties with a population of fewer than 100,000 people.

Financial Aid Application

St. Stephen's offers need-based and merit-based financial assistance to families. To apply for financial aid, families must complete an application through Clarity, our financial assistance application platform. Families must complete the Clarity application by January 20 for the priority deadline.

Texas Education Savings Account

At this time, the St. Stephen’s Episcopal School Board of Trustees and school leadership team have decided that the school will not participate in the Texas Education Savings Account (ESA) program.

St. Stephen’s is committed to accessibility, affordability, and socio-economic diversity, and we have long supported these values through a robust, self-funded financial assistance program. In the 2025–26 academic year, 22% of students received need-based financial aid awards. To support families in navigating financial considerations, St. Stephen’s offers on-campus and virtual affordability workshops, which include guidance on the financial aid application process and available payment plan options.

The Board of Trustees and school leadership team will continue to monitor the ESA program as it develops and will provide timely updates should the school’s position change. If you have questions, please contact Lawrence Sampleton, the associate head of school for enrollment management and financial aid.
