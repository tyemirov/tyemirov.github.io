Tuition & Financial Aid

We're serious about making Overlake attainable for families from all backgrounds. We offer flexible payment options and a financial aid program for eligible applicants. A diverse community makes us stronger; we welcome students from every walk of life who will contribute positively to our school.

Financial aid at Overlake covers school-related costs such as tuition, books, laptop, lunch, Project Week, and transportation, if needed.

Tuition for the 2026-2027 school year is $51,156.

If you may need financial assistance, we encourage you to apply through Clarity. The deadline for 2026-2067 financial aid applications is February 4th, 2027. The application takes less than 30 minutes to complete. If you would like to learn more about our award process and the broad range of families who qualify, please check out this guide to financial aid. 

Scholarship Opportunities

All students deserve a shot at an Overlake education.

Since 2007, Overlake has offered the Malone Scholars Program through an endowment from the Malone Family Foundation. The Overlake School is the only school in Washington to receive this endowment, which awards academically promising students the resources to attend an institution that will optimally prepare students for college and beyond.

To be eligible for the Malone Scholars Program, an applicant must:
  • Be a United States citizen
  • Meet all Overlake admissions and enrollment requirements
  • Demonstrate strong academic performance through grades and test scores
  • Strive for a college preparatory education
  • Show enthusiasm and motivation for learning
  • Have a demonstrable financial need of at least 30% of school costs
  • Be applying to or entering grades 7-12

Have questions? Reach out to our Admissions office; we're here to help.

Frequently Asked Questions

Our financial aid packages are based entirely on what families need. We don't offer grants for merit or athletics.

We use Clarity, an online platform, to help us understand your family's financial situation based on the information you share, including income, assets, expenses, and debts.

Applications submitted through Clarity require a $60 fee, but this fee is automatically waived for qualifying families. We encourage you to review our financial aid program guide for more information on how our financial aid program works, and who we serve.

If you can't complete your full application by the deadline, submit last year’s tax documents and a draft of this year’s tax return in order for the financial aid committee to provide an initial offer contingent on receiving finalized tax documents.

Tuition is only part of the cost of attendance at Overlake, and any financial aid award also includes support for the following:

  • Tuition
  • Meals and snacks
  • Project Week
  • Books
  • One-time facilities fee
  • Laptop
  • Transportation (optional)

While income is only one of the ways our financial aid committee evaluates a financial aid award, we hope that the following information illustrates that a wide range of incomes qualify for aid at Overlake.

Our financial commitment to your family is for the entire time that your child spends at Overlake. Barring a significant change in your family’s financial situation, any financial aid award will remain at a similar level.

All families receiving financial aid need to reapply each year so we can ensure the right level of support that is reflective of your family’s current needs.
