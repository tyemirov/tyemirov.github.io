Affording a Nueva Education

Nueva continues to be committed to the belief that a community of diverse voices and perspectives provides the best possible conditions for learning. We seek to attract students who reflect the diversity of the Bay Area and a variety of life experiences, interests, and talents, regardless of their economic circumstances.

Financial Assistance at Nueva

There is no way around it: a Nueva education is a significant financial investment. This is why the overriding purpose of our financial assistance program is to ensure that Nueva is affordable for every family. At Nueva, we meet 100% of a family's demonstrated financial need with grants, not loans; and, we have created a comprehensive program that accounts for the full cost of attendance. 

We want you to know that you can afford a Nueva education. 

Our Commitment to Affordability

The Bay Area's high cost of living makes tuition a stretch for many Nueva families. 

We offer 100% financial assistance grants to households earning less than $150,000 annually with assets typical of that income level. All elements of a full Nueva education–transportation, lunch, trips, enrichment activities, and after-school services, plus any school-recommended educational support–will be provided at no cost to the family. 

Additionally, households earning $150,000 to $250,000 annually with assets typical of that income level will contribute between 1 and 10% of their income towards tuition. 

Households that earn more than $250,000 and have demonstrated need will receive financial assistance. In fact, in 2026–27, such households comprise 51% of the Nueva families that receive some level of financial assistance. 

The true cost of attending an independent school goes beyond tuition, so for all families who receive financial assistance, the school will cover any additional expenses associated with a Nueva education at the same percentage as the tuition assistance each receives.

Applying for Financial Assistance

We understand that the financial assistance application process can feel challenging at times. You might have questions about how it works, what information you’ll need to share, and what documents are required. The admissions team is here to help, and we want to hear from you. 

If you are a current Nueva family applying for financial assistance for the first time for a child already enrolled at Nueva, we encourage you to reach out directly to Alex Lazo, Financial Assistance and Family Partnership Coordinator, at 650-235-7110 or alazo@nuevaschool.org.

Process Checklist

The financial assistance application process is separate and distinct from the admissions application but follows a similar timeline. All families wishing to be considered for financial assistance must complete an application through Clarity. Nueva uses the information provided through Clarity as the primary basis for determining the amount of financial assistance for which a family qualifies. 

The checklist below provides more detail about our financial assistance application process. Of course, we are also here to help, so please reach out to us directly if you have any questions!

  1. Be prepared. Gathering documents and information like your most recent tax documents can help you complete the application.
     

  2. To get started, please go to the Clarity Application and create an account. (The application typically takes less than 30 minutes to complete. The Clarity Application is also mobile-friendly, so you can complete it from anywhere. You can save your progress and return at any time.)

    • Use the dropdown menu to select The Nueva School as a school to receive your information.

    • Clarity Application Fee: At the end of the application, there is a $65 fee to submit. If the application fee is a financial hardship for your family, please email Alex Lazo, Financial Assistance and Family Partnership Support Coordinator, at alazo@nuevaschool.org for a fee waiver code. (Scroll down for information about vouchers for the IQ assessment and waivers for admission-application fees.)

    • For families with children currently in the admissions process, the financial assistance application and all supporting documents are due by February 3, 2027. (Our admissions application deadline is January 5, 2027.)
       

  3. After you complete and submit your application, please remember to return to your Clarity dashboard to upload the following tax documentation:

    • 2025 federal tax documents (Clarity will pull these documents directly from the IRS)

    • 2026 November and December pay stubs for each wage-earning parent or guardian

    • All 2026 W-2s

Please note: Returning families must submit a financial assistance application through Clarity each year. Nueva evaluates financial assistance grants annually, and grants may be adjusted depending on changes in a family's need.

Clarity offers several resources to help with the application process.

If you have questions at any point, you can email Clarity, or call Clarity at 206-210-3752.

Support Beyond Tuition

In order to enable full engagement in our wide range of school programs and activities, financial assistance at Nueva goes beyond tuition. Families that receive financial assistance will automatically qualify for a similar level of assistance for many school-related expenses, such as school-sponsored transportation, extended day, music programs, and any school-mandated tutoring and evaluations.

Nueva has a Financial Assistance & Family Partnership Coordinator who supports families receiving FA throughout their time at the school—ensuring access to all aspects of Nueva life. 

See below for information about reduced Nueva assessment fees and admission-application fees.

Vouchers for IQ Assessment, Admissions Application, and Financial Assistance Application

Applying for reduced admission-application fees is a separate process from applying for financial assistance. 

  • Ravenna Admissions Application Fee: If the application fee is a hardship for your family and you plan on applying for financial assistance, please use the code "waivefee" in the payment section in Ravenna to cover the cost. 
     
  • Clarity Financial Assistance Application Fee: If the application fee is a financial hardship for your family, please email Alex Lazo, Financial Assistance and Family Partnership Support Coordinator, at alazo@nuevaschool.org for a fee-waiver code.
     
  • IQ Assessments (grades preK–8):  Financial support is available for IQ assessments. To find out more or apply for a voucher, please complete and submit the Application for IQ Assessment Financial Assistance Voucher. Please note that Nueva vouchers are valid only for psychologists included on our Psychologist Resource List.

For information about fee waivers and IQ assessment vouchers, contact Alex Lazo, Financial Assistance and Family Partnership Support Coordinator, at alazo@nuevaschool.org.

Financial Assistance FAQs

The Nueva community strives to make education for the gifted child possible and affordable for families from diverse socioeconomic backgrounds. Currently, $9 million in need-based assistance supports 20% of Nueva students.

How do we apply for financial assistance?

The financial assistance application process, although separate and distinct, runs on a similar timeline to the admission application process. Nueva uses Clarity to help determine a family's demonstrated-need.

Be sure to note that the application deadline for financial assistance is February 3, 2027. 

What should I do if I’m a current Nueva family applying for financial assistance for the first time?

If you are a current Nueva family applying for financial assistance for the first time for a child already enrolled at Nueva, we encourage you to reach out directly to Alex Lazo, Financial Assistance and Family Partnership Coordinator, at 650-235-7110 or alazo@nuevaschool.org.

Is financial assistance available for all grades?

Yes. Financial assistance is available to applicants and current students pre-kindergarten through grade 12.

Does financial assistance cover transportation, music programs, and other non-tuition fees and expenses?

Yes. In order to enable full engagement in our wide range of school programs and activities, financial assistance helps to cover other non-tuition school-related costs. Families that receive financial assistance automatically qualify for a similar level of assistance for many school-related expenses, such as school-sponsored transportation, extended day, music programs, and school-mandated tutoring and evaluations.

 You can find  more detailed information about the comprehensive support we offer here. 

What is the typical financial assistance amount?

Financial assistance is based solely on a family’s demonstrated financial need and varies widely by situation and typically ranges from 10 to 100% of tuition.

If we receive financial assistance will we need to repay it?

No. Financial assistance at Nueva is provided in the form of grants and not loans. There is no repayment.

Do I have to reapply every year?

Yes. Financial assistance is determined annually and families must submit a financial assistance application and supplemental financial documents each year. When there is little or no change in a family’s financial circumstances, the school will renew the support at a similar level.

Could the request for financial assistance impact my child’s chances of being accepted for admission?

The admissions committee reviews applications without knowledge of financial assistance requests, and a separate committee determines financial assistance eligibility and grant levels. 

Though we do have a sizable financial assistance budget, it is finite. In some years, we receive more applications from qualified students with demonstrated need than we are able to support. As a result, there are times when we are unable to offer admission to otherwise admissible applicants simply because we cannot meet their family's financial need. 

Should we apply for financial assistance even if we’re not sure our family will qualify?

Yes. If you feel you may need financial assistance for your child to attend Nueva, you are encouraged to apply.

If we do not apply for financial assistance during the admissions process can we apply in future years?

When a family submits a "full-pay" application to Nueva, this communicates that the family will be capable of covering all educational costs for the upcoming year and the foreseeable future. Our financial assistance budget is allocated based on the information provided at the time of admissions, and families who do not apply for assistance during that process should not expect to receive assistance in future years. No funds are available for families with pre-existing financial needs that were not disclosed at the time of admission.

We understand, however, that circumstances can change. If a family experiences a significant and unexpected financial hardship, or if a change in tuition between divisions creates a new financial need, we encourage them to reach out to our Financial Assistance and Family Partnership Coordinator.

Why does each parent need to submit a financial assistance application?

The financial assistance application process asks for information about the financial resources available to each living parent and/or legal guardian. Custodial parents, non-custodial parents, step-parents, birth parents, adoptive parents, married parents, unmarried parents, remarried parents, never married parents, and legal guardians are all expected to participate in the financial assistance application process since educational expenses are considered a shared responsibility. When parents live together, a single, jointly prepared financial assistance application is typically submitted along with the required tax forms and other documents. When parents are in different households, each will need to establish a separate family account with Clarity and submit the application and supporting documentation. Please contact us in cases where a parent is completely uninvolved in the student’s life and providing no financial support.

Does Nueva offer deferred payment plans?

Nueva offers two deferred payment options—one that spreads payments over three months and one that spreads payments over 10 months. Both options involve a one-time set up fee, but neither adds interest to the amount due. The fee is waived for families receiving financial assistance.

When will we be notified about the financial assistance decision?

For families currently going through the admissions process, financial assistance decisions will be shared alongside admissions notifications from The Nueva School.

For continuing students who currently receive financial assistance, grant decisions are shared in February. Please note that incomplete applications may result in delays.

If not for donors that fund financial assistance at Nueva, I would not have been able to finish my schooling here. I feel so fortunate to have been able to attend this school and am grateful to be a part of this community every day."
