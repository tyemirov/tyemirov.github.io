TUITION FEES

At the British International School of Chicago, South Loop, our tuition fees reflect the outstanding education we offer. Your child will love learning here, inspired by world-class, highly qualified teachers and an exciting array of learning opportunities.
  2. Admissions
  3. Tuition Fees

Our state-of-the-art facilities, extra-curricular activities, and highly qualified teachers give young students the platform they need to unlock their potential. This page provides parents with full disclosure over the costs of enrolling here, with no hidden payments or extra costs.

Below you will find all the tuition fees for each year group at the British International School of Chicago, South Loop, allowing you to plan your finances in advance. You will also find details on annual fees and costs for new students. If you require any assistance meeting our school fees, you can find all the information regarding financial aid and discounts too. If there is anything you can’t find on our tuition fees page, don’t hesitate to contact us.

Financial Aid

At BISC South Loop, we value socioeconomic diversity. As such, we proudly offer need-based financial aid. Financial aid awards are based solely on financial need without regard to race, religion or ethnic background. As with all independent schools, financial aid funds are limited.

The financial aid application portal for the 2027-28 academic year is open on October 1, 2026. To begin your application please visit Ravenna Financial Aid. Here you can also check on the status of your application and submit any supporting documents. As a part of your financial aid application, we also require the submission of this Financial Aid Worksheet. Please note that the deadline for financial aid applications for new applicants is January 22, 2027.

Additional questions can be directed to our Admissions Team by email to admissions@bischicagosl.org.

 

Discounts

Tuition Discount for Northwestern University Employees

Northwestern University is recognized around the globe for its educational programs and is the No. 12-ranked school in the United States. Our South Loop campus is proud to offer all benefits-eligible Northwestern University employees with a 20% tuition discount.

More details about this discount and how to apply can be found on the Northwestern University Human Resources website.

 

Sibling Discount

We offer discounted tuition for children joining a sibling at British International School of Chicago, South Loop. The second child will receive a 3.5% discount on tuition, and any additional children will receive a 7% discount.
