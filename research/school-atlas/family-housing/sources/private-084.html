Tuition for 2026-2027

Student Type Tuition Cost Cost Includes
Boarding $81,950 Fee covers tuition, room and board
Day $57,000 Fee covers tuition, study rooms and meals at the School Monday through Saturday

Families can deposit money onto students' school-issued ID cards, which can act as a debit card for on-campus purchases, such as textbooks and supplies, personal items from the athletic store, campus center, activities, and health center charges.

There are additional fees for art courses, music lessons, driver’s education, special testing, class trips and other items. Some fees will be required in advance and others will be billed. A schedule of these fees will be supplied by the business office upon request.

Participation in the Tuition Refund Plan is required for both Boarding and Day students. The fee for Boarding students depends on the selected payment plan: the One Payment Plan is $575 or 0.7% of net tuition, while the fee for the Two Payment and Ten Payment Plans is $1,150 or 1.4% of net tuition. For Day students, the fee also varies by payment plan: the One Payment Plan is $400 or 0.7% of net tuition, and the fee for both the Two Payment and Ten Payment Plans is $800 or 1.4% of net tuition.

Blair Academy
2 Park Street, P.O. Box 600, Blairstown, NJ 07825
(908) 362-6121

Founded in 1848, Blair Academy is a dynamic, coeducational New Jersey boarding school where students in grades 9-12 pursue a superior college preparatory education grounded in warmth and academic rigor and empowered by strong faculty-student relationships and a vibrant community life.
