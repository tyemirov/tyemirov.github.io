    From Pierrepont School

    Pierrepont provides an unmatched environment for individualized learning. We are a K-12 independent school situated in Westport, CT. Our students learn in classrooms with an average student ratio of 3:1, which is lower than that of any comparable private school. Led by a world-class faculty, many of whom are practicing artists and leaders in their fields, the learning that students engage in at Pierrepont is not merely the acquisition of knowledge. It’s an experience that advances students’ understanding of themselves and the world. We are proud to foster the joy of lifelong learning.

    More About Pierrepont School Academics

    Highlights from Origins class

    Our Origins class studies Paleolithic art: the earliest evidence of symbolic thinking in humans that was found in cave paintings in France and Spain to mammoth bone carvings in Germany and Russia. Students made their own cave paintings mixing ochre paint and applying it to the walls of our "cave"-adding their marks and symbols to paintings left by several years of students.

The faculty is nothing short of exceptional; all of the teachers are true experts in their fields, offering a depth of knowledge and passion that is rare to find- nurturing intellectual curiosity rather than simply focusing on test scores or grades .

The best-in-class technology program keeps the students at the cutting edge, while the school’s college acceptance rates speak for themselves.

    Rating 5 out of 5

    Pierrepont's motto is Quid Ni?, but it might equally be IYKYK. Tuition does not fund endless sports fields, slick instagram accounts, or fancy cocktail parties where botoxed parents outdo each other with tales of vacations and renovations. Pierrepont is the antidote to all that: it offers bright and curious children a magic that can only occur when extraordinarily accomplished teachers aren't tethered to the banalities of a traditional school model. Classes, even for the youngest kids, will feel like the most inspiring university lecture you ever attended. I've never seen kids so absorbed in their learning AND so incredibly kind to one another. No exams and grades, a flexible curriculum, and incredibly small class sizes. The Pierrepont community is fascinating, diverse and quirky. While the college placement list is phenomenal, that's not the point of the school. Pierrepont won't be a fit for every child, but for the right kid, there's no better choice.
