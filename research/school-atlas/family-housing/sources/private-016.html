Providing a good education is one of the most important gifts. We know that an independent school education is an investment, the cost of which is a factor for some families in making their school decision.

Costs for the 2026–27 School Year

Day Student Tuition$68,500
5-Day Boarding Student Tuition$74,800
Approximate Additional Expenses*$1,000

*Approximate additional expenses are anticipated for miscellaneous charges such as books, athletic equipment, etc.

We actively seek to enroll excellent young scholars and are committed to ensuring that cost is not a deterrent. Nobles fulfills 100% of each family’s demonstrated need. This year, Nobles has distributed more than $9.1 million in aid to families who qualified for financial assistance, and the average grant is $53,266. Information about applying for need-based financial aid can be found below. 

Financial Aid Deadlines for New Applicants

 

January 15, 2027

Complete the Clarity application online.

March 10, 2027

Financial Aid award letters are released to families of newly accepted students.

If your family is applying for financial aid, please go to the Clarity Application and log in or create a new account. The application typically takes less than 30 minutes to complete. Please review the Clarity Family Guide in English or in Spanish. 

It is also helpful to read through the Enhanced Tax Verification (ETV) Process:
View the Guide in English, FAQ’s in English, Guide in Spanish, and/or FAQ’s in Spanish.

Please direct any questions to Director of Financial Aid, Jimmy Young at 781-320-7078 or jimmy_young@nobles.edu.  Jimmy Young is offering several lunchtime Zoom sessions for families with financial aid-related questions. Click here to join anytime between 12:00 and 12:30 p.m. on any of the following Wednesdays: 10/21, 10/28, 11/4, 11/11, 11/18, 12/2, 12/9, 12/16, 1/6 and 1/13.

For more information, visit the Nobles Financial Aid FAQs page.

Clarity customer service email address: support@clarityapp.com

 

Financial Aid Deadlines for Returning Families

 

October 15, 2026

Current families that already receive financial aid must reapply each year by submitting the Clarity application.

March 1, 2027

Re-enrollment deadline for all returning families (completed in Veracross account). 


NOTE: Families currently enrolled at Nobles wishing to apply for financial aid for the first time need to contact Jimmy Young, Director of Financial Aid, at jimmy_young@nobles.edu before submitting an application.
