# Attendance alternatives

Run `python research/attendance-modes/build.py` from the checkout root. It reads the existing canonical 100-school private catalog and `boarding-sources.json`, then writes `dist/attendance-modes.js` and `gap-report.json`.

The browser contract is `window.SCHOOL_ATLAS_ATTENDANCE = { schools: { [school_id]: { day: rate | null, boarding: rate | null } }, summary }`. Rates contain `tuition_usd`, `tuition_status`, `tuition_year`, `tuition_source_url`, `tuition_note`, recurring-fee fields, fee-completeness flags, and `boarding_schedule`. Existing cost and provenance fields are preserved for the 96 day rates and four boarding-only rates. Boarding alternatives explicitly set `cost_basis: "boarding"`, a boarding tuition basis, and null inherited annual school-cost ranges so day-rate assumptions cannot leak into the boarding calculation. A mixed school retains one school ID and its original publisher rank, with alternative attendance prices; it is never counted as two schools.

The audit adds 28 verified boarding alternatives to the 96 schools with day prices: 20 official 2026–27 published boarding prices from the current source research, Nobles and Hackley's explicitly five-day boarding prices from the cached official tuition audit, and Washington Preparatory's official optional-boarding total for **2027–28**. Hackley's price is $61,895 upper-school tuition plus $18,905 five-day boarding. Washington's $55,385 already includes the published local-student attendance components, so day fees are not added a second time. The existing four boarding-only rates preserve their own source years and fee uncertainty.

All newly added boarding alternatives keep `fees_complete: false` and `required_fee_total_unresolved: true`. Published tuition alone does not establish the complete cost of boarding: mandatory boarding charges were not fully audited, and travel is excluded. Five-day boarding retains the family home for weekends and holidays. No savings in family spending are assumed. A null boarding alternative means **not verified and priced in this audit**, rather than a conclusion that a school has no boarding offering. The gap report names those 68 schools explicitly.

The first 20 rates were supplied by the primary agent's official-source research, with their exact school URLs retained in `boarding-sources.json`. The three cached-source entries identify the cached official evidence files under `research/private-schools/tuition-audit/`. No school, rank, or day tuition in `private-school-data.js` is changed by this builder.

## Additional boarding audit

`additional-evidence.json` records a scan of all 100 existing tuition notes and live official-source checks for Cranbrook, St. Stephen’s, Village, PRISMS, and Hockaday. Five additional priced modes bring the total to 32 boarding alternatives (28 mixed plus four boarding-only). Elite’s year is unspecified: its $73,000–$84,000 occupancy package has unresolved mandatory fees, so only the $73,000 floor is usable for a bounded cost comparison. Village defaults to seven-day boarding and retains its cheaper five-day price explicitly; the two schedules are not interchangeable. Hockaday has no current boarding offer verified here. Veritas has only an undated publisher-reported boarding-price lead, recorded in the evidence report.

Boarding overlays also explicitly replace `cost_estimate_note`, `cost_estimate`, `cost_status`, and `grade_basis`, and clear `cost_range_basis`; day-only estimate descriptions cannot survive when a boarding option overlays its base school.
