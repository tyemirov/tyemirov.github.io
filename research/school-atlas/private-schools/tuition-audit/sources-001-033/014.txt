L275: # Financial Aid
L276: 
L277: ## Cost should never be a barrier to a Deerfield education.
L278: ### “Deerfield’s commitment to affordability and access in the form of need-based financial aid is historic, deep, and central to its educational mission. It is founded on the idea—beautifully articulated over a century ago when Frank L. Boyden asked families to ‘pay what you can’—that the Deerfield Experience should be accessible to all young people of promise regardless of means.”—Head of School Dr. John Austin
L279: For more than 40 percent of prospective families, applying to Deerfield Academy also means applying for financial aid. Our Financial Aid Office works with families throughout the application process and, for those who enroll, throughout their Deerfield Experience. We strive to make the process simple, streamlined, and completely confidential.

L304: Qualifying families must complete a financial aid application during the admission process. Families must live and work in the U.S. and have two years of verifiable W-2 (only) income with assets consistent with their income profile.

L306: ## Frequently Asked Questions
L307: 
L308: How much assistance will my family receive?
L309: 
L310: All families, both domestic and international, are eligible to receive up to 100% assistance.
L311: 
L312: Do you provide performance-based aid?
L313: 
L314: Deerfield provides grants based on financial need only. We do not offer financial aid on the basis of athletic and academic performance or any other merits.
L315: 
L316: What if my family's financial situation changes?
L317: Financial Aid grants are reviewed annually. Once admitted, we strive to assist a family at a consistent level throughout their time at the Academy. If things change and a family has an unexpected circumstance (job loss, divorce, death in the family, etc.) we review each case on an individual basis and, budget permitting, adjust accordingly. Likewise, we will decrease grants when families have a change in circumstance that allows for an increase in their contribution amount.
L318: Will applying for financial aid affect my admission to Deerfield?
L319: 
L320: Possibly. Deerfield has a very generous financial aid budget. However, it is limited, and therefore we cannot always accept all admissible financial aid applicants.
L321: 
L322: Will Deerfield require a family to take out loans?
L323: 
L324: No. Deerfield grants are outright awards based on financial need. There is no repayment required.
L325: 
L326: Does Deerfield provide financial aid for International Students?
L327: Yes. If admitted we meet the full demonstrated need of international students, up to 100% of tuition.
L328: 
L329: Does Deerfield provide assistance beyond tuition grants?
L330: 
L331: Yes. Some students receive assistance with additional expenses such as airfare, health insurance, music and dance lessons as well as athletics and global studies travel.
L332: 
L333: What does Tuition include?
L334: At Deerfield, tuition includes the cost of attending the Academy (room and board), textbooks, use of a MacBook Air, and use of the Academy’s health center.
L335: 
L336: My family owns a business and we do not have W-2 income, are we still eligible for financial aid?
L337: 
L338: Yes. All families are eligible for financial aid and can apply through Clarity Financial Aid.
L339: 
L340: Are all parents required to submit a financial aid application?
L341: Yes. All legal parents/guardians, including step-parents must complete a financial aid application.  We understand each family’s situation is unique.  Please reach out if you have specific questions about your situation.

L389:

L392: ## New Student Applications
L393: #### Instructions
L394: 
L395: All new financial aid applicants must submit their application(s) to the Clarity Financial Aid platform: 【126†https://auth.clarityapp.com/en/signup/†auth.clarityapp.com】
L396: 
L397: U.S. Tax Filers Only: As part of the Clarity Financial Aid application process (with authorization using the IRS 4506C tax release document), Clarity will pull your 2025 1040 tax return and 2025 W2s directly from the IRS and import the documents directly into your application.
L398: All applicants must use the exact same name that they use on their admission application.  Please do not use nicknames.
L399: 
L400: More information about how to apply through Clarity may be 【127†found here†drive.google.com】.

L402:

L405:   * International (Non-Canadian) Applicants: Current Bank Statement, Salary Verification or Non-U.S. Tax Filing must be uploaded by January 15, 2027

L451: 
L452: In what grade can I start my application?
L453: 
L454: Deerfield welcomes applications for students currently in 8th-12th grade who are applying to grades 9th-12th or a postgraduate year for the following academic year.
L455: 
L456: What can I do if I am not in 8th grade yet?
L457: 
L458: Current 6th graders and beyond may 【45†request our viewbook†deerfield.schooladminonline.com】 to be entered into our system for future application years.
L459: Prior to 6th grade, we encourage you to explore our website to familiarize yourself with Deerfield and all it has to offer.
L460: 
L461: When can I visit campus?
L462: 
L463: 7th graders can visit campus April-August. Dates may vary, but traditionally these appointments are offered on weekdays.

L497: Do you provide application fee waivers?
L498: Deerfield does provide application fee waivers for qualifying families. To be considered for a fee waiver, applicants who have completed their Candidate Profile must submit our 【124†Fee Waiver Request Form】. This is a financial need based process.

L500: What financial aid does Deerfield offer?
L501: Deerfield’s financial aid program is a need-based program that supports 100% of demonstrated need. The program is comprehensive and may cover up to 100% of the total cost of tuition. We also support additional expenses such as travel and health insurance for a portion of our community.

L508: Financial aid awards are sent on March 10.
L509: 
L510: What is your policy on late applications?
L511: 
L512: Deerfield accepts students for September enrollment only. Our priority application deadline for September enrollment is January 15. We will make every effort to fully consider applications completed (including testing) after January 15, however we cannot guarantee a March 10 notification.
L513: Admission opportunities for late candidates, who do not receive a March 10 notification, is limited by the number of spaces available after April 10. The Admission Committee meets periodically throughout the spring to make decisions on this group of candidates.
