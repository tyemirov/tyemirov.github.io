L94: Providing a good education is one of the most important gifts. We know that an independent school education is an investment, the cost of which is a factor for some families in making their school decision.
L95: ###
L96: ## Costs for the 2026–27 School Year
L97: 
L98: Day Student Tuition  | $68,500
L99: 5-Day Boarding Student Tuition  | $74,800
L100: Approximate Additional Expenses*  | $1,000
L101: 
L102: *Approximate additional expenses are anticipated for miscellaneous charges such as books, athletic equipment, etc.
L103: We actively seek to enroll excellent young scholars and are committed to ensuring that cost is not a deterrent. Nobles fulfills 100% of each family’s demonstrated need. This year, Nobles has distributed more than $9.1 million in aid to families who qualified for financial assistance, and the average grant is $53,266. Information about applying for need-based financial aid can be found below.
