L115: # Tuition & Affordability
L116: 
L117: * * *
L118: ## We encourage all interested families to apply.
L119: 
L120: * * *
L121: 
L122: We recognize that an independent school education is a significant financial commitment, an investment that lasts a lifetime. To ensure that Castilleja is affordable for all admitted students, we offer a robust Tuition Assistance program that meets the needs of a wide range of financial circumstances.
L123: ## Tuition Assistance
L124: 
L125: Our goal is to support families who cannot pay for the full tuition at Castilleja. Through an annual budget of over $4 million, 1 in 5 students receives tuition assistance. It is important to know that applying for tuition assistance has absolutely no impact on admission decisions.
L126: By determining what each family can contribute to tuition, the Committee allocates awards ranging from $4,490 to $67,700, depending on each applicant's financial situation. Recipients of Tuition Assistance represent a broad range of income levels.

L139: ## 【57†Application Process】
L140: 
L141: Our Tuition Assistance Committee reviews all completed tuition assistance files and makes decisions based upon each family’s demonstrated need using calculations from the tuition assistance application hosted by 【62†Clarity Tuition†app.clarityapp.com】. The Committee may require additional documentation, such as bank statements and monthly budgets, if more information is needed in order to understand a family’s financial situation.
L142: A family needs to submit an tuition assistance application and required documentation to 【62†Clarity Tuition†app.clarityapp.com】 (more information below) by the deadlines listed below:
L143: 
L144:   * November 2, 2026 - Current Families
L145:   * January 5, 2027 - Applicant Families
L146: 
L147: If the $65 application fee presents a financial hardship for your family, please don’t hesitate to email tuitionassistance@castilleja.org or call Laura Martinez at (650) 470–7732 to request a fee waiver.

L190: ## 【60†Policies and Principles】
L191: 
L192: Castilleja considers tuition assistance to be the single most important vehicle to maintain an economically diverse student population. We recognize the increasing burden tuition places on families and the tuition assistance committee works to ensure that Castilleja is an affordable option.
L193:   * Tuition Assistance is distributed to qualifying families of students in grades 6-12. The School strives to meet a family's need as determined by 【62†Clarity Tuition†app.clarityapp.com】 and all other supporting documentation.
L194:   * Tuition Assistance awards do not require repayment. However, recipients must reapply for tuition assistance each year. If a family's financial situation hasn't changed the percentage of tuition covered by the award remains at the same level from year to year, and the payment amount adjusts to reflect the annual increase in tuition. Only in a few cases, when a family experiences a significant change in financial circumstances, will the percentage of tuition assistance be adjusted.
L195:   * A supplemental student activity and support budget exists to enable students to participate in all aspects of school life on an equitable basis.
L196: 
L197:   * Castilleja will give priority to families who have previously received aid prior to making any new commitments.
L198: 
L199:   * When a parent does not work and there are no preschool children or aging parents at home, the school will impute an annual income of at least $30,000 to the non-working parent/guardian.
L200:   * Parents with legal parental rights must submit all of the required financial aid application documents in order for their child to be considered for a tuition grant.
L201: ## 【61†Confidentiality Statement】
L202: 
L203: Families can be assured that all information concerning financial aid is held in the strictest of confidence by Castilleja School. Only those persons directly involved in the financial aid decision-making process or in its administration will review this information.
