L464: # Tuition & Financial Aid
L465: ## Affording Loomis Chaffee
L466: 
L467: At Loomis Chaffee we are committed to enrolling the most outstanding applicants and creating the best possible educational opportunities for them, regardless of their ability to pay.
L468: 
L469: Operating as a tuition-free school until the 1960s, Loomis has remained accessible for students from a wide range of socioeconomic backgrounds due to our unwavering commitment to financial aid.
L470: For the 2026–27 year, Loomis awarded $14.2 million in financial aid to 33 percent of our student body. Financial aid is intended to make the cost of attending Loomis affordable. To that end, aid grants range from $1,000 up to the full cost of tuition, with the average grant covering 80 percent of tuition.
L471: 
L472: The school also hosts a financial aid lending library for textbooks and other supplies, which helps to reduce the cost of book purchases for all students on financial aid.

L487: ## 【188†Explanation of Fees】
L488: ### Technology Fee
L489: The Technology Fee is a usage fee that covers the cost of data access, ongoing improvements to the school information system, and the cost of all networks used by students.
L490: Services include but are not limited to faster bandwidth; wireless connectivity around campus; computing facilities in the library and other locations; Internet and email access; web portals for students and parents that provide access to grades and comments, school calendars, directories, and news; an emergency notification system; and software including Microsoft 365 Office account and Adobe Creative Cloud.
L491: In addition, the Information Technology Department can assist with warrantied repairs on MAC and PC computers.
L492: ### Health Center Fee
L493: 
L494: The Health Center Fee covers services provided by the Health Center to students, such as those services rendered by the nurses on duty, a nurse practitioner, a medication nurse as needed, and other staff both day and night. The fee also covers basic health assessments and remedies administered by the center including first aid and emergency care. The fee does not include diagnostic testing such as flu, strep, mono, COVID, etc.
L495: This fee does not cover insurance. All boarding students must provide proof of health insurance coverage or elect to enroll in the Student Accident and Sickness Insurance Program. More information about this program is available on the  【191†Health Center page】.
L496: ### International Fee
L497: 
L498: The International Fee helps cover the range of services unique to students who live abroad. These costs include (but are not limited to) the advising, administrative, and programming work of the Office of International Student Support as well as the safekeeping of documents such as passports and I-20s. All students who require a Form I-20 from the school as well as any student who elects to receive services from the Office of International Student Support will be charged this fee.
L499: ## 【189†Additional Expenses】
L500: 
L501: Books, field trips, and various athletics supplies are additional. Annual cost of books, art supplies, and other academic materials usually range from $1,200 to $1,600 depending on the courses taken by the student. Other optional costs include laundry service, music lessons, and academic tutoring.
L502: 
L503: Students will need to bring a computer to school; the school will provide the Microsoft 365 Office software package for all students who need it.
L504: ## 【190†Payment Plans & Schedule】
L505: 
L506: At Loomis Chaffee, we recognize that you are making a significant financial commitment to your child’s education and that this may be a challenge for families. To help you manage those costs, we offer several payment options.
