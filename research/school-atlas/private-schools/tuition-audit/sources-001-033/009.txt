L581: ## 【277†Payment Options】
L582: 
L583: All tuition and related fee billing is processed through FACTS Tuition Management (FACTS). After completion of the Hotchkiss enrollment contract, families will receive an invitation from FACTS to register and select their payment plan for the year.
L584: 
L585: Once you have created your login credentials, you should 【280†use this link†online.factsmgt.com】 to access your account.
L586: Payments from U.S. bank accounts can be made by check to FACTS Tuition Management or by electronic payment from your checking or savings account.
L587: 
L588: International payments must be made in U.S. dollars using the FACTS platform. The Hotchkiss School and FACTS have partnered with Flywire and Convera to streamline this process.
L589: 
L590: Late fees are assessed at 1.5% per month on past-due balances that are 30 days past the due date.
L591: If you have questions regarding the outlined payment options, please contact student accounts (studentaccounts@hotchkiss.org) at (860) 435-3165.
L592: ## 【278†Tuition Refund Insurance】
L593: The 【281†Tuition Refund Insurance Plan】 is mandatory for all students unless payment is received for the full year’s tuition and fees by June 1. This plan insures a portion of the tuition in the event of certain absence or separation from the School according to the terms of the policy. You authorize the School to collect any claim payment to which the School is entitled under the plan and to credit your child’s account, paying any excess to the School.
L594: An absence or separation covered by the terms of this plan does not relieve you of your obligation to the School. You agree to pay the School the balance due, if any, after the application of all plan payments to your account, and we agree to refund the excess, if any, to you.
L595: The cost of the plan is 1% of the net tuition. Your student will automatically be enrolled if you select either the 2-installment or 10-installment payment plan. If you select the pay in full option, you can elect coverage when you choose your payment plan.
L596: ## 【279†Questions?】

L598: Questions or concerns regarding any of the outlined payment plans should be directed to student accounts (studentaccounts@hotchkiss.org) at (860) 435-3165.

L621: ## 【287†Will insurance from outside the U.S. be accepted?】
L622: 
L623: Local healthcare providers require insurance with a United States-based location for claims submission. If your enrollment contract was completed with an international address (outside of the 50 United States), your student was automatically enrolled in the International Injury and Sickness Insurance (ISI plan) made available through the School that meets this requirement and provides comprehensive coverage from a domestic insurer.
L624: ## 【288†Will a travel insurance policy be accepted?】
L625: 
L626: No, a travel policy does not provide the comprehensive injury and sickness coverage required.
L627: 
L628: ## 【289†Can we provide a credit card to pay for medical expenses instead of insurance coverage?】
L629: 
L630: No, most healthcare providers require insurance to ensure payment, and are not willing to accept a credit card as a payment method to establish care for a student.
L631: ## 【290†If our insurance plan only covers emergency care in Connecticut, will that be sufficient?】
L632: 
L633: A comprehensive plan that covers local providers, including specialities such as orthopedics, surgeons, and physical therapy, is required to ensure students can receive any necessary care and follow-up while at school.
