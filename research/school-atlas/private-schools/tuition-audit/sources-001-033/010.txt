L90: ## Tuition for 2026-27 (Grades 7–12)
L91: 
L92: Stanford OHS has three different tuitions, each corresponding to one of our three enrollment levels.
L93: 
L94: ENROLLMENT STATUS  | ACADEMIC YEAR TUITION
L95: Full-Time (4 or more courses)  | $31,900
L96: Part-Time (2–3 courses)  | $19,140
L97: Single Course   | $6,380
L98: 
L99: Tuition does not cover books, course materials, or extracurricular expenses for event attendance, yearbooks, etc.
L100: Note: Single course enrollment is defined as one year-long course. Two semester-long courses taken in different semesters would be viewed as one course for the purposes of tuition. Single course tuition is also applicable if the student takes only one semester-long course.
L101: ## Additional Expenses
L102: ### Books and Materials
L103: 
L104: The total cost of books and materials will depend on the number of and the particular course(s) the student is taking. The costs of books and materials vary widely by course. For example, the materials for a science course for which the student needs to purchase both a book and a lab kit will be more expensive than the digital books needed for some English courses. For an individual student, total books and materials may cost between $30 and $1,300.
L105: Students who are awarded financial aid for academic year tuition will also receive financial assistance towards books and materials.
L106: ### Electronic Devices
L107: 
L108: Students must attend Stanford OHS courses from a computer, not from a phone or a tablet. Any mid-range computer released in the last two years should be sufficient for successfully participating in live classes. For some math and science courses, students may need a graphing calculator and/or a graphics tablet.
L109: ### Internet
L110: 
L111: A positive learning experience at Stanford OHS requires a stable broadband Internet connection that can support video conferencing. Our instructors use Adobe Connect, a feature-rich platform, to provide an engaging learning experience for students. Students will be simultaneously on camera, watching presentations/videos, and messaging the class, all of which require a high internet bandwidth for each student.
L112: # Optional In-Person Events
L113: While the expenses above are required for full engagement in classes, Stanford OHS also offers a variety of opportunities to engage as a community. Most of these are free or low-cost, including clubs, online assemblies and workshops, and local meetups. Other large in-person events are optional to attend, but do require event tickets and travel. Many students and families typically attend one major event per year at their discretion.
L114: Prices below reflect ticket prices and do not include travel expenses for students and their accompanying family members.
L115: Financial aid recipients can receive discounts on event tickets.
