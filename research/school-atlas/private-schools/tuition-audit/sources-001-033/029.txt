L56: ## Tuition
L57: 
L58: Tuition reflects small classes, expert faculty, and an individualized approach for intellectually curious students with growth mindsets.
L59: 
L60: * * *
L61: 
L62: Sibling Discount
L63: We believe in family and support keeping siblings together. We offer a sibling discount for families with more than one student at the school. ​Tuition will be reduced by:
L64: 
L65:   * 10% for the second (younger) child
L66:   * 20% for third and additional younger children enrolled
L67: ​
L68: ## 2026-2027 tuition:
L69: ​1st - 3rd Grades:     $26,750
L70: 4th - 8th Grades:   $30,750
L71: ​9th - 12th Grades:  $32,750
L72: ​
L73: 
L74: * * *
L75: 
L76: Tuition FAQs:
L77: What does tuition include?
L78: 
L79:   * ​Small classes
L80:   * Expert faculty
L81:   * Hands-on activities
L82:   * Engineering
L83:   * Computer Science
L84:   * After-school activity period from 4:15-5:15pm
L85:   * Field trips
L86:   * Technology (Windows laptops for Upper Elementary to Upper School). Students may also bring their own device.
L87: 
L88: How often do you raise tuition? ​
L89:   * ​We strive to keep tuition increases modest and predictable for our families. For historical reference, the tuition when we opened in the 2015-2016 academic year was $24,500 for 4th-7th grade.
L90: ​
L91: Do you offer financial aid?
L92: 
L93:   * ​We are evaluating implementing a needs-based financial aid program for future years to help families.
L94: ​
L95: ​Do you have payment plans or fees?
L96:   * Yes, parents may pay in one lump sum, 2 equal payments, or 10 installments.
L97:   * There is a $250 fee for the 2-payment plan and $500 for the 10-payment plan.
