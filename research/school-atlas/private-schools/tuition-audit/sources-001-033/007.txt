L419: ### HOW TO APPLY FOR FINANCIAL AID
L420: 
L421: Harvard-Westlake partners with Clarity Financial Aid to process financial aid applications. When you click the link below, you will be prompted to create an account. After that, you will fill in your information and an evaluation will be made. Although Clarity collects information and documents, grants and eligibility are determined by the Financial Aid Committee here at Harvard-Westlake.

L474: TUITION PAYMENT PLANS
L475: ### TUITION PAYMENT PLANS
L476: 
L477: Harvard-Westlake offers the following payment plans for families:
L478: 
L479:   * Annual: Pay in one installment, due on July 1, 2026
L480:   * Semiannual:  Pay in two installments, due on July 1, 2026 and January 1, 2026
L481:   * Quarterly: Pay in four installments, due on July 1, 2026; October 1, 2026; January 1, 2027; and April 1, 2027
L482:   * Monthly: Pay in 10 monthly installments, commencing July 1, 2026 and concluding April 1, 2027
L483: Please contact Student Billing at studentbilling@hw.com or 818-487-5426 if you have any questions.
