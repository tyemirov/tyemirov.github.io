L30: Vanessa Cruz Santana ’06 knows firsthand what it means to invest in a Cate education. An alumna of the school, Vanessa arrived to the Mesa in 9th grade through the 【2†A Better Chance program】. L31: ### How Awards are Calculated
L32: Our financial assistance committee administers need-based awards and follows the guidelines of the National Association of Independent Schools (NAIS). Financial assistance decisions are based on a family’s ability to finance their child’s education as demonstrated by the methodology of 【3†Clarity†app.clarityapp.com】. Within the limitations of our budget, and according to our own financial assistance policies, Cate uses Clarity recommendations as a guideline in determining individual awards.
L33: Although the admission and financial assistance programs at Cate are separate processes and an applicant must first be admitted to Cate and only then be considered for financial assistance. We notify all new students and their families of our financial assistance decisions at the same time that we notify them of our admission decisions.

L42: ### Domestic Applicants: Filling Out The Clarity Application
L43: Clarity is a comprehensive online form that allows Cate to estimate the amount your family can contribute towards your child’s tuition.  Clarity reports that estimate to any school or schools you indicate on your application. If your child is applying to more than one school, you only need to fill out your Clarity application once. If you have multiple children applying, only one application is needed for all schools for all of your children. The application itself is also fully translated into Spanish.
L44: The Clarity application is due December 1, 2026, for returning families and is due February 1, 2027 for new prospective families. To begin your financial assistance application for the coming school year, please follow these steps:
L45: 
L46: 1. Visit the 【3†Clarity†app.clarityapp.com】 website.
L47:   *     * A login and password are required to complete the application. If you are a first-time user, you will be asked to create an account.
L48:     * Begin your application for the 2027-28 academic school year.
L49:     * The application typically takes less than 30 minutes to complete. The Clarity Application is also mobile-friendly so you can complete it from anywhere.
L50:     * You can log out of the Clarity website at any time and return later to complete the application.
L51: 2. Submit your completed Clarity application.
L52: 
L53:   *     * Please note that there is a non-refundable processing fee. If you qualify for a fee waiver, you will be automatically notified on the processing fee page.

L55: 3. Upload required documents in the “Document Requests” section of your Clarity dashboard. Required documentation includes:
