L236: ## Hero

L240: # Our Approach
L241: 
L242: ##### We want the world to be here.
L243: 
L244: Our families come from everywhere–different countries, regions, cultures, backgrounds, and life experiences–and that's exactly what makes Choate, Choate.
L245: ##### We will ensure the tuition and financial aid process is clear and affirming.
L246: 
L247: We will be present for every step, all the way through. From your first question to your final decision, we're your partner– here to explain, clarify, and support at each part of the process.
L248: ##### We encourage you to apply for financial aid.
L249: 
L250: Financial assistance is simply part of how our families join the Choate community. We hope you won’t hesitate to apply, and we are here to help get you started.
L251: 
L252: We want you to see the full Choate, and we want to get to know your full self, independent of financial circumstances

L272: ## Additional Costs
L273: 
L274: Students may be eligible for assistance with the following additional costs:
L275:   * ‌ Books and Academic Supplies - Families should budget $1,000 for books, supplies, and incidental charges annually.
L276: 
L277:   * ‌ Health Insurance - Choate requires all students to carry health insurance.
L278: 
L279:   * ‌ Cell Phone - Choate requires that all students possess a cellular phone with a U.S. data plan.
L280: 
L281:   * ‌ Travel to and from Campus - Families should budget for transportation costs, including during major school breaks when School is closed.
L282:   * ‌ Music Lessons - Choate offers music lessons whose cost varies with the frequency and duration of participation.
L283: ## Payment Plans
L284: 
L285: ##### One Payment
L286: 
L287: This plan is for families who would like to make one payment for all yearly tuition and fees on or before June 1. Families utilizing this option will receive a 1% discount on net tuition and fees less the non-refundable deposit.
L288: 
L289: Please contact studentbilling@choate.edu or (203) 697-2536 if choosing this option.
L290: ##### Two Payments
L291: 
L292: This plan requires families to pay 50% of net tuition and fees by July 15 and the remaining balance of net tuition and fees by November 15.
L293: ##### Nine-Month Payment Plan
L294: 
L295:   * Enrollment begins in April. The nine-month payment plan allows you to spread your payments over nine months and runs from July to March.
L296:   * An automatic deduction occurs on the 1^{st} or 15^{th} of each month.
L297:   * Enrollment by June 15 is required.
L298:   * The payment plan is not a loan, so there are no credit checks or interest charges.
L299:   * Your payments are auto-debited from your bank account monthly.

L309: ## Beyond the Classroom Fund
L310: 
L311: Every Choate student – whether or not they receive financial aid – can apply to the Beyond the Classroom Fund. Its purpose: to make sure every student can fully participate in the Choate experience.
L312: 
L313: We understand school doesn't stop at tuition. Trips, gear, materials, and the everyday costs of academics, athletics, and extracurriculars all add up – and this fund exists to ease that burden, so cost never gets in the way of opportunity.
L314: The Fund can also offer support in moments that call for it most. At the dean of students' discretion, a student facing unexpected or emergency circumstances is encouraged to apply for this support.
L315: ## Apply for Financial Aid
L316: 
L317: 🗓️The deadline to apply for Financial Aid is January 15.
L318: 
L319:   * 【100†Step 1: Complete the Financial Aid Application】
L320:   * 【101†Step 2: Submit Supporting Documents】
L321: ## 【100†Step 1: Complete the Financial Aid Application】
L322: 
L323: Choate uses the 【102†Clarity Application】 to determine a fair and equitable grant for every family.
L324: Choate meets 100% of a family’s demonstrated financial need. In determining a financial aid award, the Financial Aid Committee takes into account not only tuition charges and related fees, but normally anticipated costs such as books, supplies, laundry, and travel. The committee also considers extenuating circumstances that may affect the family’s contribution.
L325: Students with exceptionally high levels of demonstrated need receive additional financial assistance with the cost of books, travel, supplies, and other needs.
L326: ## 【101†Step 2: Submit Supporting Documents】
L327: 
L328: Families filing U.S. tax returns:
L329: 
L330:   * If self-employed, submit proper business tax return information.
L331:   * Families who have Schedule E income from a partnership and/or S-Corporation are asked to submit 1065 and/or 1120-S paperwork along with corresponding K-1 information.
L332: 
L333: Families who do not file U.S. tax returns:
L334: In completing your 【102†Clarity Application】, please be prepared to upload the previous year’s official income/asset verification forms and additional documents, including:
L335: 
L336:   * Income tax returns
L337:   * Wage statements
L338:   * Official documentation from an employer.
