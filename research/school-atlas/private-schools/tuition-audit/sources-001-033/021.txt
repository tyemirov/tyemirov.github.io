L421: ## Affording an RCDS Education
L422: 
L423: Rye Country Day School's extensive financial aid program is core to who we are. The school grants approximately $8.1 million in need-based financial aid to approximately 15% of students.
L424: RCDS offers financial aid to qualifying families with students entering Kindergarten through Grade 12. Please note, we do not offer financial aid for Pre-Kindergarten. Our financial aid program is need-based, which means the School does not offer academic, athletic, or merit-based scholarships of any kind. Financial aid is granted to families of qualified admissions candidates who demonstrate financial need.
L425: The amount of financial aid each family receives is determined based on a third-party assessment of how much each family can reasonably be expected to contribute towards their children's education. Grants range from a partial to full (100% tuition and fees covered) depending on each family's need.

L483: ### Why is the financial aid program so important to RCDS?
L484:   1. We believe that a socio-economically diverse student-body is a hallmark of a Rye Country Day School education that directly benefits all students.
L485: 
L486:   2. We believe that a child's access to enroll at Rye Country Day School should not be determined on the basis of a family's ability to pay tuition.
L487:   3. Our promise to support and care for every student and family in our school extends to issues around financial aid, which includes but is not limited to tuition grants. We ensure that students who receive financial aid are able to participate fully in the life of the school.
L488: RCDS is fortunate to have the support of alumni, parents, and benefactors who believe in the critical importance of a robust financial aid program. As a result, the school has built an endowment of approximately $99 million. In addition, the School takes in roughly $5-6 million each year in annual fund contributions. Tuition derived income covers a majority of the School's operating expenses.
L489: The funding derived through annual giving and from interest earned on the endowment closes the gap between the tuition-derived income and the full cost of operating the school.

L511: ## How to Apply for Financial Aid
L512: 
L513: Please indicate on your application for admission that you are interested in applying for financial aid. Email feewaiver@ryecountryday.org to receive a code to waive the admissions application fee.
L514: 
L515: 
L516: 
L517: To apply for financial aid, we ask that parents/guardians 【165†complete a financial aid application through Clarity†app.clarityapp.com】, an external organization that assists with determining families' financial need.
L518: Please note: The financial aid process and the admissions process are separate. You will receive notification at the same time of both the admissions decision and your family's financial aid grant.
