L243: # Tuition and Financial Aid
L244: 
L245: Since our founding, Commonwealth School has been dedicated to increasing access and welcoming as many qualified students as possible through extensive need-based financial aid. Making tuition more affordable and within budget for our families continues to be an institutional priority. This empowers us to attract strong applicants from a wide range of backgrounds and to compose a well-rounded and talented student body.

L275: ## Financial Aid Grants and More
L276: 
L277: We remain committed to partnering with families to meet the cost of a Commonwealth education. Commonwealth’s financial aid includes textbooks, biannual all-school trips, and participation in clubs and sports. We also offer supplemental grants for transportation and technology; additional scholarships for summer programs and school-sponsored international trips; discounted standardized testing, test preparation, and tutoring; and flexible payment plans.
L278: Applying for financial aid at Commonwealth takes less than thirty minutes.
L279: ## Flexible Payment Plans
L280: 
L281: All Commonwealth families have three payment plan options; they select one annually that works best for their budget:
L282: 
L283:   * Ten Monthly Payments (from May through February)
L284:   * Two Payments (by June 1 and October 1)
L285:   * One Payment (by June 1)
L286: 
L287: Families of returning students should consult the 【76†Parent Portal】 (login required) for information that is specific to them.

L309: ## Access and Affordability
L310: 
L311: All students can avail themselves of all opportunities at Commonwealth through additional grants for technology, transportation, and international travel, as well as sliding-scale costs for tutoring, standardized tests, and more.
L312:   * Technology grants are made available to eligible families and can be applied to purchasing a MacBook Pro laptop, printer, and software through Commonwealth and at a reduced cost. There is no cost to the families of students with a financial aid percentage of 90 or higher. For the 2026–2027 school year, on average, families paid $185 for this package.
L313:   * Transportation stipends offset some or all of the cost of commuting to Commonwealth via the MBTA.
L314:   * Chatfield Cultural Scholarships help cover student travel and exchanges, as well as travel-related independent projects.
L315:   * Discounted professional tutoring, standardized tests, and test preparation are also available to students with financial aid grants.
