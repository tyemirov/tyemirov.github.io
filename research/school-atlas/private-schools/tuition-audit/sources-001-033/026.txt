L115: The Spence School has a strong and longstanding commitment to enroll students with great potential regardless of their family’s financial circumstances.
L116: Tuition for the 2026-2027 school year is $71,185.00. Tuition includes all program costs, including books, class trips, and meal services. Currently, one in five Spence students receive Financial Aid, and the School allocates $8.5 million annually to need-based Financial Aid to make Spence accessible to a range of families.
L117: ## Tuition 2026-2027
L118: 
L119: $71,185
L120: 
L121: The tuition is inclusive of all program costs, including books, class trips, and meal services. The only exceptions are nominal fees:
L122: 
L123: Parents’ Association Dues (per family): $50
L124: 
L125: Class Dues (per child): $75
L126: 
L127: Registration Fee: $75
L128: 
L129: Payment Options
L130: 
L131: After the Initial Deposit of 20%
L132: 
L133: Annual: June 1st 80% Tuition
L134: 
L135: Semi-Annual: June 1st 70%, November 1st 10%
L136: 
L137: Monthly - May: December over 8 months (80%)

L161: Spence processes its financial aid application through Clarity. 【87†Clarity†app.clarityapp.com】’s application is available in both English and Spanish. The application takes less than 30 minutes to complete and is mobile-friendly. The application fee is $65, and you may share your application with additional schools that accept Clarity at no additional charge.
L162: Clarity offers a 【88†step-by-step guide†www.clarityschools.com】 to support families through the process, and you may email questions to support@claritytuition.com in both English and Spanish.
L163: Learn how to get started with the Clarity Quick Reference Guide in 【89†English†spenceschool.myschoolapp.com】 and 【90†Spanish†spenceschool.myschoolapp.com】.
L164: The deadline for the financial aid application is December 1, 2026.
