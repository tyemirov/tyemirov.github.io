Tuition and Financial Aid
making a delbarton education accessible to qualified young men
Delbarton is committed to making an independent Catholic high school education available to young men throughout the tri-state area, regardless of background. With $4.32 million granted each year in grades 9-12, our financial aid program often makes Delbarton LESS expensive to attend than other tuition-charging schools in the area. Tuition is virtually all-inclusive so that there are no additional costs for books, lunch, activities, or athletics.
*Please note: Delbarton does not award merit scholarships (academic, leadership, or athletic).  We do not offer financial aid to families in the Middle School (grades 7 and 8).
INTERESTING FACT: Our generous financial aid program often makes Delbarton LESS expensive to attend than area tuition-charging schools.
2026-27 Tuition:
$51,500
Tuition is virtually all-inclusive, including books and lunch.  The only cost not covered by tuition is optional daily contracted transportation to and from school for students who do not receive bus transportation from their local school board or who come to school by car.  The cost per rider for an annual subscription is $7,000 for morning and afternoon busing, except for pickup from Morristown Train Station, which is $4,000.
Delbarton Financial Aid Myths & Facts
applying for financial aid
All financial aid awards are based on a family's demonstrated financial need, as determined by an independent financial aid assessment service (
Clarity
). No academic merit, leadership or athletic scholarships are awarded at any time.  Please also note that we do not offer financial aid to Middle School students.
All aid is awarded as grants that do not require repayment.  Families applying for financial assistance can also request a school application fee waiver.
The financial aid application process is confidential, secure and convenient. Awards remain with a family for all years a student is enrolled, unless a family does not reapply for financial aid, there is a significant change in the family’s finances, or the student’s academic or disciplinary record is unsatisfactory.
Financial aid applications must be started and completed no later than December 1, 2026.

Tuition Refund Plan
The Tuition Refund Plan
is in use at over 1,200 leading independent schools and colleges.
Your financial obligation to Delbarton is for the full annual tuition as stated in the school's enrollment contract.  The school cannot refund tuition or cancel unpaid obligations. The optional Tuition Refund Plan provides substantial insurance protection to safeguard your financial commitment against unforeseen circumstances.
The annual cost of the plan per student is $585 for the 2026–27 school year.
Tuition Financing
Your Tuition Solution
offers convenient payment plans. You can use this link to see all payment options with no credit impact until you select a plan and move forward.
Delbarton School does not sponsor and/or endorse information from specific loan programs; however, as a convenience to families considering a loan, Your Tuition Solution is a private loan program for those individuals who prefer to pay their tuition expenses over an extended period of time. Parents and guardians are free to select loan programs from any institution.
