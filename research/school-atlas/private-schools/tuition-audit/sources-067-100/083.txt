Tuition Information
Crossroads’ tuition includes all field trips, Experiential and Outdoor Education (EOE) trips, athletic team fees and numerous other expenses. We strive to keep our tuition rates affordable for more families and are committed to providing over $11 million through our Affordability Program to one in four students. We are grateful for our generous community’s annual support of the Crossroads Fund, which provides critical funding for our Affordability Program and makes our extraordinary academic and extracurricular programming possible.

Affording Crossroads
Affordability Program
We believe a Crossroads education should be accessible to families across all income levels. We realize that the cost of tuition is beyond the reach of many families, so the School has established our Affordability Program, which grants over $10 million annually and benefits one in four Crossroads students.
