Affording Pingry
We support bright, promising students regardless of their financial circumstances.
A Pingry education makes an immediate impact and has lifelong value. We want that education to be accessible to families from a broad range of backgrounds. That’s why we offer a generous tuition assistance program to support students and families with financial need.
We recognize that a Pingry education is a significant investment and do not want tuition to be a barrier for entry. We believe that socio-economic diversity enriches our community and is critical to our mission to innovate, launch world-changers, and foster values like compassion, curiosity, and awareness of self and others.
We encourage our prospective families to apply for tuition assistance if they are concerned about affordability.
We are excited to partner with you!
2027-28 Tuition Assistance Applications are now open
Independent School should be affordable, and applying for aid should be easy: contact us with tuition assistance questions at any time.

Payment Plan Options
For 2026-2027, three payment options are available to you:
Pay tuition and fees in full
by August 1, 2026.
Pay in two installments:
70% paid by August 1, 2026 and the remainder by February 1, 2027.
Ten-month payment plan
, paying 15% of tuition and fees for each of the first five months with the first payment due by May 1, 2026, and 5% of tuition and fees for each of the remaining five months.
Along with these plans, families have additional options for managing their accounts, including:
A wider choice of payment methods including direct debit, credit card, and paper check, either as one-time or automatically recurring payments, and the option to save your methods of pay for future remittance
The ability to access their account 24 hours a day electronically via computer or mobile device.

Tuition Refund Insurance
All Pingry families have the choice to  enrolled in The Tuition Refund Insurance provided during the enrollment process. The Tuition Refund Insurance will pay benefits (subject to its terms, conditions and limitations) to the family of a student who, for various reasons, is unable to continue attending the school.

Additional Expenses Support Structure
First-Tier Expenses
Expenses covered by Pingry at the same percentage as a student’s financial aid percentage (Eg. Lunch, busing, global program trips, extracurricular trips, and textbooks).
Academic Trips & Global Programs:
If selected for the program/trip, a student’s financial aid percentage applied to the total cost of the trip.
Extracurricular Trips:
If selected for the program/trip, a student’s financial aid percentage applied to the total cost of the trip. Extracurricular trips include:
Sports team trips (international and domestic).
Music group trips.
Student club trips (Model UN, for example).
Busing:
Student’s financial aid percentage applied to the total cost of daily transportation.
Textbooks:
Student’s financial aid percentage applied to the total cost of required textbooks during the academic year.
Second-Tier Expenses
Expenses covered by Pingry at the same percentage as a student’s financial aid percentage, but capped at a certain amount (mandatory athletic apparel/spirit wear and technology grant).
Athletic Apparel & Spirit Wear:
Student’s financial aid percentage will be applied to the total cost of
mandatory
athletic apparel/spirit wear.
Student’s financial aid percentage applied to the total cost of mandatory athletic apparel/spirit wear. Students will have a maximum amount that will be covered for each sport/season based on their financial aid percentage.
Technology Grant:
Rising Middle and Upper School students receiving tuition assistance who do not have a laptop that meets the
minimum requirements
for school use may apply for a one-time (per Division) technology grant.
If eligible, a student’s financial aid percentage will be applied to the total cost of the student's laptop. Students will be eligible for one laptop in Middle School and one  in Upper School. Any portion of the laptop cost not covered by the technology grant will be billed to the student’s account and subject to Pingry's regular payment policies.
The laptop will be purchased through the school; however, ownership of the laptop will be transferred to the family upon summer pick-up in the Tuition Assistance Office. After pick-up, the laptop will be owned and maintained by the family and not be the property of the school.
If your child does not have a laptop that meets these requirements for the 2026-27 academic year, the technology grant application will open on April 26, 2026.
Third-Tier Expenses
Expenses that are mandated by the institution and covered at the same percentage as a student’s financial aid percentage (tutoring and medical evaluations).
Tutoring:
Student's financial aid percentage applied to the total cost of tutoring that is deemed mandatory by department chairs and/or division directors.
Counseling & Medical Evaluations:
Student’s financial aid percentage applied to the total cost of evaluation. This applies to evaluations that the school is mandating for a specific student (academic, psychological, counseling, physical, etc.).

Tuition Assistance Guiding Principles
We support bright, promising students regardless of their financial circumstances.
A Pingry education makes an immediate impact and has lifelong value. We want that education to be accessible to families from a broad range of backgrounds.
Pingry’s commitment to excellence includes building a diverse school community across all demographic measures. This helps create a multidimensional educational experience for our students and helps prepare them to be lifelong learners, global citizens, and honorable leaders. That’s why we offer a comprehensive tuition assistance program to support students and families with financial need.
Impact on applicants
Pingry’s Tuition Assistance Program is designed to provide access to a broad group of applicants.
Impact on Pingry students
We are committed to financially supporting Pingry students from all backgrounds in a manner which allows them to take full advantage of the extraordinary resources available at Pingry, to develop a strong sense of belonging, and to thrive academically.
Need-Based Tuition Assistance
The Pingry School offers tuition assistance to families with demonstrated need - the difference between what a family can afford to pay as determined by the proprietary formula applied by
Clarity Tuition
, a third party tuition assistance application provider for independent schools, and the estimated total cost of attendance.
Pingry strives to meet
up to 100% of the demonstrated need for each student we enroll and support
. However, the School recognizes that what a family may experience as their financial need is sometimes different than that determined by the tuition assistance formula.
How to apply for tuition assistance
For all students:
Please complete your Tuition Assistance application using
Clarity Tuition
.
To get started, please go to the
Clarity Application
and create an account. The application typically takes less than 30 minutes to complete. The
Clarity Application
is also mobile-friendly so you can complete it from anywhere. You are also able to save your progress and return at any time. At the end of the application, there is a $65 fee to submit, and you can share your application with additional schools that accept the
Clarity Application
for no additional charge. Simply use the dropdown menu in the application to select those schools.
Tuition Assistance Award Determination
After a family completes their application on the
Clarity Tuition platform
, our Tuition Assistance committee uses its own policies to determine the most equitable financial aid grant. In determining an award, the Tuition Assistance Committee takes into account not only tuition charges and related fees, but normally anticipated costs such as books, transportation, meals, and travel. The committee also considers extenuating circumstances that may affect the family’s contribution.
Tuition Assistance Appeal Process
If a family wishes to appeal their tuition assistance award, they must put a request in writing to the
Director of Enrollment Management
. In order for the Tuition Assistance Committee to review such requests, a family must provide additional information concerning circumstances that might impact the calculation of their need.
