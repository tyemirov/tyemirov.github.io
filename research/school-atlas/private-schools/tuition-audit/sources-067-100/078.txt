Tuition & Tuition Assistance
Educational Impact
Unlock Potential Without Limits
Crystal is a significant investment; we recognize this. Incorporating diverse experiences and perspectives into a collective learning journey can significantly enhance an education. This is why over 22% of our students benefit from our comprehensive Tuition Assistance Program (TAP), with awards ranging from $1,000 to full tuition. On average, these awards cover 72% of tuition costs.
TAP extends to all aspects of school life, including books, computers, athletic gear, BART buses, and school-sponsored events like homecoming, prom, and graduation ceremonies. This ensures every student can immerse fully in the Crystal experience without financial constraints.
We don't offer merit scholarships; instead, we focus solely on need-based assistance. This approach ensures that all our students, regardless of their financial background, feel equally cherished, supported, and empowered to thrive.
Explore Your Options
If you're considering Crystal but are concerned about affordability, we encourage you to explore our tuition assistance options and payment plans. Your child's growth and individuality are worth the investment.

Make Crystal Affordable
Step 1
Go to
https://app.clarityapp.com/
sign-up
.
Step 2
Begin your application and add Crystal Springs Uplands School to your student applicant entry. You can save your application and log out at any time and return later to complete it.
Step 3
Enter your financial information, review your application, and pay the $65 Clarity application fee. If you need a fee waiver, please email us at
affordability@crystal.org
.
Step 4
Complete the Clarity Enhanced Tax Verification (ETV) process to release your W2 and personal tax return transcripts from the IRS. You'll be asked to authorize the sharing of your tax transcripts and verify your identity. This is a one-time security measure required by the IRS to protect against identity theft and the compromise of personal information. Only those filing US taxes will be prompted to complete this step. Please review the
step-by-step walkthrough
of this process.
Important Considerations when submitting the Clarity Application:
Please submit one application per household. Every parent or guardian in the applicant's life, including biological, step-parents, and non-custodial parents, must complete an application through Clarity if living in a separate household or filing separately. The first household can start the application and invite the second household to participate. Information is confidential between households. We understand unique circumstances and are open to considering exemptions when necessary.
Our tuition assistance is an annual commitment. We reassess every family's financial need each year, so reapplication is necessary. Awards evolve with tuition adjustments and consider changes in financial circumstances, including the number of children attending tuition-charging schools.
Family Information Webinars
Clarity, the platform used for the Tuition Assistance (Financial Aid) application, hosts several Application Info Webinars for families. During the live session, Clarity representatives will go over some key aspects of the application, things to look out for, materials to gather, how to get help, how it works, and a robust Q&A. Typically these will run for about 60 minutes but can last longer if there are lots of questions.

Your Questions Answered
List of 3 items.
Does seeking tuition assistance affect my child's admission chances?
Your child’s potential is our primary focus. We evaluate all applications on merit without considering financial need. While final decisions consider available funds, our commitment remains to make Crystal accessible to all deserving students.
What if we miss the January application deadline?
Unforeseen circumstances can occur, and as such, late applications may be considered, subject to the availability of spaces in the corresponding grade and at the discretion of our Director of Enrollment Management and Affordability.
Will my child be labeled if we receive tuition assistance?
No. Every student’s journey is personal and confidential. The tuition assistance process remains discreet, ensuring no student or faculty member knows who receives assistance.

Crystal's Commitment to Anti-Racism
Nondiscriminatory Policy:
Crystal Springs Uplands School admits students of any race, color, national and ethnic origin to all the rights, privileges, programs, and activities generally accorded or made available to students at the school. It does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national and ethnic origin in administration of its educational policies, admissions policies, scholarship and loan programs, and athletic and other school-administered programs.
Website Accessibility Statement:
Crystal Springs Uplands School is committed to making this website accessible to visitors with disabilities and is continually working to increase its accessibility and usability. We are on a regular basis seeking opportunities to bring all areas of the site up to the same level of overall web accessibility. If for any reason, you cannot access any of the material on our website, please email webadmin@crystal.org and we will work to resolve the problem as soon as possible.
