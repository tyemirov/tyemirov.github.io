Book Fees
Early Learning and Lower School
- All the required books and supplies for students in the Early Learning Campus (ELC) and Lower School (LS) are included in the Book Fee. Textbooks are rented and must be returned to the classroom at the end of the school year; all other materials are purchased and may be taken home. Unreturned or damaged rented books will be charged to the student’s account. The fees are as follows:
PK3 - PK4
$240

$355
1st Grade - 5th Grade
$485
CP - CM2
$405
Middle and Upper Schools
- Due to multiple elective options, Middle School (MS) and Upper School (US) students in both programs must order their books and materials in the spring. Orders will automatically be charged to the student’s account and will be invoiced in September 2026 by the Business Office.
MS and US books range in price depending on the grade and the electives.

Uniforms
All students must wear the school uniform. The average cost is between $200 - $250. Students are required to have a full dress uniform as well as a daily uniform.
Bus Service
Awty contracts with AFC Transportation to provide bus service to and from the school. Buses pick-up and drop-off riders at designated points. Bus fees for 2026-2027 are $3,500 for round-trip ridership and $1,750 for one-way ridership. Availability of bus service is subject to a minimal number of riders; therefore, changes (including cancellation or price adjustments) may be made to the routes based on ridership.
Optional Programs
Language Classes: Depending on enrollment, some language classes, such as Arabic, German, Hindi, Mandarin, and Russian, may be self-financed.
