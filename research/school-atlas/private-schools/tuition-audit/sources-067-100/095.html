Annual Giving at Eastside Prep

Tuition covers 92% of the costs associated with a student’s EPS experience. The remaining 8% of educational costs are funded by contributions to the EPS Annual fund. These contributions are voluntary and everyone is asked to participate in the Annual Fund by making a gift that is meaningful to their family. In 2025- 2026, the Annual Fund has provided approximately $2,700 per student to cover the total cost of their education. Families unable to donate that amount are asked to donate what is possible, while other families contribute beyond the $2,700.

As of February, 95% of EPS parents have contributed to the 2025-2026 Annual Fund.

Eastside Prep does not host fundraising events—no auctions or fundraising dinners. Our EPS community events are designed for us to have fun and get to know each other.

In 2023, our 20th anniversary year, Eastside Prep announced and began raising funds for our endowment, signaling financial maturity and health as an institution. Once these funds generate annual investment income, they will support professional development and student financial aid.

Health Office Information

As a reminder, even if you have consented for your student to self-carry medications, it is required by state law that a physician/ provider sign the medication authorization form. Drop Off Emergency or Prescription Medications (as needed) – August 15
Families are invited to drop off emergency medications (EpiPens, inhalers, etc) at the front desk of the TALI building. Medications should be securely bagged with student name and grade clearly marked. A second set of medications must be provided for student athletes to travel with the coach/trainer. Medications provided to the school may not expire during the current school year.

Does your student have life-threatening allergies, asthma, seizures, or another unique medical condition? If so, and you would like to meet with the school nurse to discuss your student in more detail, please feel free to email Health Services Coordinator Krista Kelly at kkelly@eastsideprep.org. We can set up a time to meet in person, over the phone, or via email. Each student’s medical needs are unique, and open communication/ thorough care planning helps us create a safe and supportive environment for every student. I look forward to hearing from you!

For students with medications held at school (EpiPens, inhalers, etc.): Please be sure to check the expiration dates on all medications to ensure that they are not past the expiration dates and will not expire during the school year.

If your student cannot self-carry their EpiPens or inhalers (or if you would like to have a backup set kept at EPS), those items can be dropped off at school during Conference Days or prior to the first day of school at the TALI front desk. Items should be placed in a Ziploc bag and clearly labeled with your student’s name and grade. Remember to include a second set if your student is participating in sports. These will be kept with the coach/athletic trainer.

If you would like to discuss your student’s allergy/ medical condition in more detail, please feel free to reach out to Krista Kelly, Student Health Coordinator and School Nurse, to schedule an in-person or virtual meeting. 

Students who are not authorized to self-carry medications will not be able to attend school until emergency medications are dropped off. 
Questions?  Contact School Nurse Krista Kelly.

While medical forms are not due until July 31, families are strongly encouraged to schedule any needed doctor’s appointments for physical exams and/or immunizations soon as summer appointments fill quickly. ALL students must have their medical questionnaire and health profile completed by July 31. This is done through GE and is valid for one school year.

Physical exams: All students participating in sports (including Middle School sports that practice during the school day) need a valid physical. Physicals are valid for two years from the date of the exam listed on the form. Physical exams are due before the first practice, and the athlete will be ineligible without a current exam form uploaded in GE.

Applicable action plans (for asthma, allergies, seizures, diabetes, or other life-threatening conditions) must be completed and uploaded in GE by July 31. Because students with an action plan cannot safely start school without a plan telling us what to do in the event of an emergency. action plans are required before the first day of school. Please email School Nurse Krista Kelly with any questions. Action plans are valid for one school year.  

Families should also consider having their medical provider fill out a Medication Authorization Form. This form is required by Washington State law if students need access to any over-the-counter or prescription medications at school or on field trips. Medication Authorizations are valid for one school year.

Incoming 7th graders are required to have a DTAP booster prior to starting school. Please contact your pediatrician to verify that your student is up to date. The booster needs to have been given after the age of 10.

All required forms must be submitted prior to July 31 in order to receive student schedules and book lists. Contact School Nurse Krista Kelly with any questions.
