Annual Giving at Eastside Prep
Tuition covers 92% of the costs associated with a student’s EPS experience. The remaining 8% of educational costs are funded by contributions to the EPS Annual fund. These contributions are voluntary and everyone is asked to participate in the Annual Fund by making a gift that is meaningful to their family. In 2025- 2026, the Annual Fund has provided approximately $2,700 per student to cover the total cost of their education. Families unable to donate that amount are asked to donate what is possible, while other families contribute beyond the $2,700.
As of February, 95% of EPS parents have contributed to the 2025-2026 Annual Fund.
Eastside Prep does not host fundraising events—no auctions or fundraising dinners. Our EPS community events are designed for us to have fun and get to know each other.
In 2023, our 20th anniversary year, Eastside Prep announced and began raising funds for our endowment, signaling financial maturity and health as an institution. Once these funds generate annual investment income, they will support professional development and student financial aid.

Health Office Information
As a reminder, even if you have consented for your student to self-carry medications, it is required by state law that a physician/ provider sign the medication authorization form.
Drop Off Emergency or Prescription Medications (as needed) – August 15
Families are invited to drop off emergency medications (EpiPens, inhalers, etc) at the front desk of the TALI building. Medications should be securely bagged with student name and grade clearly marked. A second set of medications must be provided for student athletes to travel with the coach/trainer. Medications provided to the school may not expire during the current school year.
Does your student have life-threatening allergies, asthma, seizures, or another unique medical condition?
If so, and you would like to meet with the school nurse to discuss your student in more detail, please feel free to email Health Services Coordinator Krista Kelly at
kkelly@eastsideprep.org
. We can set up a time to meet in person, over the phone, or via email. Each student’s medical needs are unique, and open communication/ thorough care planning helps us create a safe and supportive environment for every student. I look forward to hearing from you!
For students with medications held at school (EpiPens, inhalers, etc.):
Please be sure to check the expiration dates on all medications to ensure that they are not past the expiration dates and
will not expire during the school year.
If your student cannot self-carry their EpiPens or inhalers (or if you would like to have a backup set kept at EPS), those items can be dropped off at school during Conference Days or prior to the first day of school at the TALI front desk. Items should be placed in a Ziploc bag and clearly labeled with your student’s name and grade. Remember to include a second set if your student is participating in sports. These will be kept with the coach/athletic trainer.
If you would like to discuss your student’s allergy/ medical condition in more detail, please feel free to reach out to Krista Kelly, Student Health Coordinator and School Nurse, to schedule an in-person or virtual meeting.
Students who are not authorized to self-carry medications will not be able to attend school until emergency medications are dropped off.
