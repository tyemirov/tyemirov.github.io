Tuition & Affordability
We know a Bryn Mawr education is a significant financial commitment for any family. It is also an investment in your daughter’s academic and personal development that pays dividends over her lifetime as she pursues higher education, a fulfilling career and most of all, a considered and consequential life.

Payment Schedule
Families may select to pay tuition in one, two, or ten-month installments through the Blackbaud Tuition Management program. Payment schedules are as follows:
One Installment: 100% of tuition less the Reservation Fee is due August 1, 2026.
Two Installments: 65% of tuition less the Reservation Fee is due August 1, 2026. The remaining 35% is due on December 1, 2026.
Ten Installments: Monthly payments are due August 1, 2026, through May 30, 2027.
Monthly Payment Plan
Bryn Mawr uses Blackbaud Tuition Management (BBTM) for all tuition payments and student billing, which may include Brynmawrket purchases, activities fees, and academic materials/equipment when applicable. During enrollment, you will be asked to verify your household and student information and select a payment plan. Families who select the 2- or 10-month installment plan for tuition are subject to an annual administration fee of $56 per family. After registration, you will be able to access your Tuition Management account directly from the Bryn Mawr Parent Portal. You can view your billing information, payment history, invoices and more from within your account.
Tuition Refund Plan
Parents should consider taking advantage of the Tuition Refund Plan option noted on the enrollment contract. This plan protects tuition fees in the event of a student's withdrawal from school. A brochure outlining the details of this plan is mailed with the enrollment agreement and available on the Parent Portal.

After School Care
Bryn Mawr offers care until 5:30 p.m. through Lower School Extended Day and Middle School Afternoon Supervision programs. Costs vary by division and are billed on a monthly basis. Families may opt for a full-year contract or pay for drop-in care. Registration for after school programs opens in late summer.
Flexible Tuition Program
The cost of educating a student at Bryn Mawr–with our personalized attention, stellar faculty, state-of-the art facilities and myriad opportunities beyond the classroom–is substantial. We recognize that the tuition and fees can feel prohibitive to many families and we are committed to making Bryn Mawr accessible and affordable for the family of each student we admit regardless of financial circumstances.
In return, we seek families who will place the educational experience of their child as a priority in budgeting their financial resources and in their commitment of time.
Who Should Apply?
We value a diverse school community that includes students with a variety of economic backgrounds. We offer tuition payment options and award approximately $7 million in tuition assistance each year to more than one-third of our families. Flexible Tuition awardees include middle-income families, families with relatively high income who have multiple children in private schools, and those whose financial circumstances qualify them for a grant near the full cost of tuition and fees.
Families interested in the Flexible Tuition program submit information about their financial resources to Clarity and we use standard criteria to evaluate a family’s ability to pay the tuition. While it is difficult to define a “typical” Flexible Tuition candidate, common threads among families who qualify are factors like income, the number of children in a family, and lifestyle choices.

Divorced Parents Policy
Both parents must fill out a Parents' Financial Statement. We will consider the assets of both parents, natural and/or adoptive, if living, before making any award. If the custodial parent has remarried, the committee will consider the assets of the stepparent while keeping in mind the stepparent's obligations to his or her own children.
Parent, Guardian and Step-Parent Policy
In accordance with our policy concerning parents, guardians, and stepparents, please provide the Bryn Mawr Flexible Tuition Program with complete income information by submitting the financial information on one Clarity application or by submitting individual Clarity applications for each parent, guardian and stepparent.

Bryn Mawr School
Merit Scholarships
The Bryn Mawr School is pleased to provide both merit and need-based scholarships to qualifying students who embody the values and mission of The Bryn Mawr School.
Head of School Awards
New Middle and Upper School students receive consideration for a Head of School Award, a partial tuition scholarship. Award recipients are chosen based on exceptional academic performance, strong personal integrity, leadership potential, and significant involvement in the community. All applicants who submit their
admission application
by the deadline will automatically be considered for one of these awards, which are communicated with admission acceptance. The Head of School Award is renewable annually, provided the student continues to demonstrate high academic achievement and solid character.
Named Scholarships
Each year, a number of Middle and Upper School students are awarded named scholarships honoring their exceptional academic potential and outstanding personal qualities. These students benefit from financial support provided by funds created by long-time supporters and friends of Bryn Mawr. Named scholarship recipients must
apply
and qualify for need-based financial aid through our regular Flexible Tuition Program. Incoming student recipients will be notified with their acceptance communication.
