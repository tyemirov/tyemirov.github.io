WA Prep Merit Scholarship

At Washington Preparatory School, we believe in recognizing and nurturing exceptional academic talent. That’s why we are proud to offer the Waprep Merit Scholarship – a prestigious award designed to support and encourage students who demonstrate outstanding academic achievements and potential.
This merit based scholarship is our way of acknowledging the hard work, dedication, and brilliance of high achieving students. Recipients of this scholarship are chosen based on their exemplary academic records, outstanding talent, and potential to contribute positively to our school community and society at large. Approximately 60% of applicants receive a merit scholarship, reflecting how many of our students are recognized for their achievement.

Merit Scholarship Requirements

Full Tuition Scholarship Eligibility:

  • Earn straight A’s in all courses across BOTH semesters.
  • Attain a standard test percentile of 98% or above.
  • Receive an award in a national-level competition.

Half Tuition Scholarship Eligibility:

  • Secure straight A’s in ALL courses across BOTH semesters.

25% Tuition Scholarship Eligibility:

  • Obtain a minimum of 6 A’s in ALL courses across BOTH semesters.
  • Ensure no grades lower than a B in any courses.

The School Board may also consider additional scholarships or financial assistance on a case-by-case basis. Families interested in learning more are encouraged to submit an inquiry to info@waprep.org.
