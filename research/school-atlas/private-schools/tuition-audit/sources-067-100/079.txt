Scholarships & Fees
UWC-USA works closely with families to provide as much need-based aid as possible, so every student has the opportunity to embrace diverse perspectives and develop transformational leadership skills, regardless of financial background.

LEARN MORE
Davis Scholarship
All students who are US citizens applying to any UWC through the U.S. national committee are automatically considered for the merit-based Davis Scholarship. A limited number of full-tuition places are available at UWC-USA for students who are not awarded a Davis Scholarship.
U.S. Citizens Receive $25,000 per Year
Davis Scholars receive a merit award of $25,000 per year applied toward tuition regardless of which UWC they attend.
Twenty-Five Places for UWC-USA
These scholarships are limited and competitive. Twenty-five places are reserved for students to attend UWC-USA
Two Places Reserved for Other Campuses
Typically two scholarship places are reserved for US students at each of the other campuses.

Tuition Costs
All UWCs work collaboratively with U.S. families to award as much need-based aid as possible. At UWC we believe every family should pay what they can, but no family should pay what they cannot.
Cost of Attendance
UWC-USA tuition for the 2027-2028 academic year is $59,800. The cost of attendance at any of the other 17 schools varies considerably, ranging from just under $25,000 to a little over $75,000 per year, depending on economies, exchange rates, and government policies related to funding in different countries.
Need-based Financial Aid
School and Student Services is an organization that calculates family contributions by examining personal assets, incomes, and tax histories.
Scholarships
For U.S. Davis Scholars, cost of attendance is reduced by $25,000 per year, and to cover any balance, students are eligible to apply for need-based aid.
Beyond Tuition Costs
Students who qualify for significant need-based financial assistance may also qualify for travel reimbursement, spending money, and other miscellaneous expenses.
