See every Perspective

A pre-kindergarten through grade 12 private school with campuses in Fort Lauderdale and Boca Raton, Florida

Where perspective shapes possibility.

Nurturing emergent leaders…

We send Pine Crest graduates into the world as emergent leaders who know their strengths and have the courage to challenge norms, break barriers and move forward with confidence.

Learning Through Performance

The stage becomes a classroom where students learn by doing, developing their craft through rehearsal, collaboration and performance.

Ideas in Motion

Purpose-built entrepreneurship spaces mirror the collaborative environments students will encounter beyond Pine Crest, giving them access to emerging technology and spaces to meet, develop ideas, present their thinking and turn possibility into action.

Built for Growth

Our athletic facilities become classrooms of their own, where students learn through practice and competition, developing discipline, teamwork and resilience along the way.

Designed for Discovery

Purpose-built innovation spaces give students the freedom to design, build, code and experiment, learning through the process of turning ideas into something tangible.

Learning Takes Root

In our organic gardens, the natural world becomes a living classroom where students explore sustainability, ecosystems and agriculture through observation, experimentation and hands-on learning.

Stories Take Shape

Professional-level studios and production technology give students the tools to report, produce and create across broadcast journalism, film and digital media, turning ideas into stories for real audiences.

Room to Wonder

Our libraries invite students to read deeply, ask questions and follow their curiosity, providing spaces for research, reflection, collaboration and discovery.
