Who Should Apply for Tuition Assistance?

Tuition and Tuition Assistance Explained

All tuition assistance, which is Woodberry's term for financial aid, is based on a family's need. We consider a wide range of factors, including family income and how many siblings a boy has. Woodberry is committed to making sure its private boarding school opportunities are available to all deserving boys, and that's why the school meets 100 percent of a family's financial need when it admits a boy who requires tuition assistance.

Because the actual cost of educating each student exceeds that of our tuition and fees, every Woodberry student, in effect, receives aid — the difference between the tuition and fees and the actual cost of a Woodberry education.

Our substantial tuition assistance budget comes entirely from many generous alumni, parents, and friends. In recent years, 46 percent of our students have received assistance. An example of the range of grants is $8,100 to $72,300. 
 
We award $8,975,050 in tuition assistance annually to many middle-income families, families with relatively high incomes but more than one child in school, and those whose financial circumstances qualify them for a grant that equals the full cost of tuition and fees. The average grant is $50,140. Even if you're unsure if you qualify for assistance, we encourage you to apply.

Applying for tuition assistance has absolutely no effect on a student’s application for admission. Admission decisions are based on a student’s qualifications and the likelihood that he will contribute to the school's life. A family’s application for tuition assistance is considered separately from the application for admission. Woodberry's tuition assistance program is open to students of any race, creed, and national or ethnic origin. 
  
Tuition
The tuition for the 2025-2026 school year is $72,300. This price includes room, board, and all school activities. Unlike many other schools, there are no activity fees at Woodberry.

Tuition Assistance FAQs

List of 7 frequently asked questions.

  • Q: Will my family qualify for assistance?

    A:
    Even if you're unsure if you qualify or assistance, or are full-pay at your child's current school, we encourage you to apply!

    Tuition assistance at Woodberry Forest is need-based. Eligibility depends on numerous factors, including income, assets, parents’ age, other financial obligations, and the number of dependents with the family. Finances should never deter a family from applying to Woodberry.

  • Q: How does Woodberry determine a family's demonstrated need?

    A:
    The school follows guidelines set by Clarity for financial aid, in conjunction with the National Association of Independent Schools. Clarity uses a formula that takes into account the costs of housing, food, clothing, transportation, insurance, and other non-discretionary expenses. This allows  Clarity to calculate how much a family can afford to spend on a child's education.

  • Q: What types of assistance are available?

    A:
    Woodberry offers tuition assistance in the form of grants, not loans. These grants are gifts from our endowed funds to ensure that Woodberry is accessible to all qualified applicants. Woodberry does not offer merit-based scholarships.

  • Q: Do I need to apply for tuition assistance each year?

    A:
    Tuition assistance is awarded on an annual basis and families must submit an application each year so awards may be calculated.

  • Q: Does Woodberry offer a payment plan?

    A:
    Yes, the school offers several options that allow tuition payments to be made for up to nine months.

  • Q: Beyond the cost of tuition, what other expenses might my family have to pay?

    A:
    Woodberry’s tuition is comprehensive and covers the full cost of attendance. Woodberry does not charge fees for playing sports or other activities. Travel and book expenses are essentially the only necessary additional cost beyond tuition and vary based on where a young man is from. Woodberry does consider travel and book expenses when making tuition assistance grants. 

  • Q: Who can I reach out to with additional Tuition Assistance questions?

    A:
    Please email your questions to tuition.assistance@woodberry.org
