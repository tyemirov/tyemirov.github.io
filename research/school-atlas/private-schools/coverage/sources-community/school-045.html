Financial Aid at CA

The 2026–27 budget for Concord Academy’s financial aid program is $6.7 million, which represents 22% of the school’s tuition revenue. CA provides all financial aid as grants; there are no loans to repay.

Financial aid grants currently support 100 new and returning students, which represents approximately 23% of the student body. Grants range from $7,400 to full tuition. Financial aid funds are also provided to help defray the cost of additional expenses including books, travel, athletic equipment, computers, school trips, music lessons, tutoring, and the college application process.

Financial Aid Eligibility

If you feel you need financial assistance for your child to attend Concord Academy, we encourage you to apply. All financial aid funds are disbursed on March 10; therefore, we are unable to consider financial aid applications submitted after the January 15 deadline. Families who anticipate needing financial aid at any point during their child’s enrollment at CA should apply for financial aid when applying for admission since there is no budget for returning students not previously funded.

This chart gives a general sense of what families from different income brackets can expect from CA’s financial assistance program. Many factors, including family size, sibling tuitions, assets, and home equity, are considered in addition to household income when CA determines financial aid grants.

Applying for Financial Aid

1. Visit the Clarity website to begin your financial aid application.

2. Set a username and a password that will allow you to return to your financial aid application at a later date before submitting it.

3. Refer to Clarity’s quick reference guide for an overview of the steps to complete your financial aid application.

Fee waivers for the financial aid application are automatically applied to families who meet certain income guidelines. All parents (including spouses, if remarried) are required to complete a Clarity financial aid application by January 15. Applications completed after this deadline cannot be guaranteed for consideration.
