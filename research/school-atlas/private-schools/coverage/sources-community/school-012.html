ADMISSIONS

Founded in 1856, St. Paul’s School is a 100% boarding high school where 538 students and 112 teachers live and learn together as they pursue excellence in character and scholarship. Our diverse student body comes from 37 states and 30 countries, and nearly 40% receive financial aid. U.S. students in families with annual household incomes up to $150,000 generally qualify for full tuition support.
