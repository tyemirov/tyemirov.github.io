Tuition

Tuition, Fees, and Other Costs

Hero Treatment

Taft offers exceptional academic and co-curricular programming, and retains some of the most talented faculty in their fields. The historic buildings on our beautiful 226-acre campus include both state-of-the-art and sustainable technologies, designed to be environmentally and fiscally responsible while providing comfort and security for our students. We balance this with our desire to keep Taft affordable. Our tuition does not cover the full cost of a Taft education; the difference is made up through our endowment funds and gifts.

2026-27 Costs
Boarding Tuition (including room and board) $81,400
Day Tuition $60,050
Books and spending money (estimated) $2,000

A deposit is due upon enrollment.  Taft offers three payment plan choices outlined in the enrollment process.
Please note that we do not charge any additional fees such as a technology fee, graduation fee, etc.
