Affording Newark Academy

During the 2026-2027 school year tuition is $55,525 plus $2,350 in required fees. However, 20% of students pay far less than that. We believe financial circumstances should never stand between a talented student and an extraordinary education.

  • Newark Academy meets 100% of demonstrated financial need for all admitted families
  • We use Clarity to ensure an accessible, fair and streamlined process
  • Financial aid covers more than tuition, ensuring full access to trips, athletics, and school life

All Financial Aid is Need-based

 

Newark Academy meets 100% of demonstrated financial need for all admitted families.

We Partner With Clarity

 

We use Clarity to evaluate financial need and ensure an accessible, fair and streamlined process.

Financial Aid Covers More Than Tuition

 

Financial aid covers more than tuition, ensuring full access to trips, athletics, and school life.

Financial Aid Factors

 

Clarity considers a family’s income, assets, expenses, debt, and other factors. Completed applications are reviewed by Newark Academy’s Financial Aid Committee, and decisions are communicated in the spring for the upcoming school year.

Costs Beyond Tuition

Newark Academy monitors the total cost of attendance, and aims to minimize costs beyond tuition for all families. For families receiving financial aid, NA provides proportional aid on all required items, including lunch and technology fees, transportation, books, Immersion and Capstone experiences. Each student receiving financial aid also receives a discretionary allowance to help offset additional school-related expenses.

Our Signature Scholarship:

The Newark Scholars Program

Newark Academy continues to honor and celebrate its long-standing history and connection with the city of Newark. NA established the Newark Scholars Program in 2012 to provide full scholarships to up to six students each year who live in or attend school in Newark. NA remains proud of its ties to Newark and seeks to provide talented students from our namesake city with the very best independent school education.

If you live in or attend school in the City of Newark and complete the Admission and Financial Aid processes, you will be considered for the Newark Scholars Program, which covers the cost of tuition, fees, trips and transportation. 

In addition to financial support, being part of the Newark Scholars program also includes:

  • A dedicated Program Coordinator who serves as a resource for Scholar recipients and their families
  • A mentorship program, which pairs new and returning scholars 
  • Participating in the Newark Scholars Cohort, which meets monthly
  • An active alumni network with on campus opportunities for current scholars to meet with alumni

What are the tuition and fees for the 2026-27 school year?

  • Tuition: $55,525
  • Lunch Plan: $1,950*
  • Technology Fee: $400*
  • Matriculation Fee (first year only): $100*

*mandatory fees

Does Newark Academy offer financial aid and scholarships?

Yes. Newark Academy provides more than $5M in need-based financial aid annually to ensure that Newark Academy remains accessible and affordable to families with varying financial circumstances.

What type of financial aid does Newark Academy offer?

All financial aid at Newark Academy is need-based. NA does not offer merit-based scholarships. Need is defined as the difference between the cost of attendance and the family’s ability to pay, which is calculated through Clarity, a third-party provider. To be eligible for financial aid, parents must complete a financial aid application and provide financial documentation through Clarity.

When are tuition payments due and are there payment options available?

There are three payment options available:

  • Single Payment: Tuition and mandatory fees paid by July 15, 2026.
  • Semi-Annual Payment: 70% tuition and fees paid by July 15, 2026 and 30% by December 15, 2026.
  • 10-Month Payment Plan: Due the 10th of each month from May 2026 - February 2027

What if I am a single parent?

Unless a parent is deceased, Newark Academy expects that both biological parents contribute to a student’s tuition expenses. Therefore, financial information is required from both biological parents. Financial aid may not be granted if data from only one biological parent is provided. If one parent is not in contact with the student and has no role in the student’s life, Newark Academy will request the custodial parent complete a waiver as part of the financial aid application and awarding process. If a biological parent does not complete the financial aid process, they will not be given access to or receive any information from Newark Academy about their child.

What if I am remarried?

The income and assets of stepparents will be taken into consideration when determining a student’s financial aid grant. Newark Academy will also take into consideration the stepparent’s financial obligation to his, her, or their own biological children. We reserve the right to decline aid packages to families who do not provide income and asset information of stepparents.

What if I or my spouse do not work?

Newark Academy will impute wages of $30,000 annually for any non-working parent who has a child over the age of five. Consideration is made in the case of extenuating circumstances, for example, if the non-working spouse is responsible for a special needs child and/or elderly parent(s). All extenuating circumstances must be documented, in writing, at the time of application.

Do I need to apply for financial aid each year?

Yes. Newark Academy requires that the Clarity financial aid application is submitted annually for all families requesting consideration for need-based financial aid. All applicants must provide all supporting documents directly to Clarity by the stated deadlines. Financial aid applications received after the deadline may not be considered. Completed applications are reviewed by the Financial Aid Committee. Awards are granted in the spring and are for one year only. 

What other fees do I need to pay?

Newark Academy has made the monitoring and limiting of costs beyond tuition an important institutional priority. NA provides proportional aid on all required items, including lunch and technology fees, books, Immersion and Capstone experiences. For non-required items, the school’s budget has been expanded in order to absorb incidental costs traditionally borne by families. Each student receiving financial aid will receive an allowance from NA’s discretionary budget to use at their discretion for other school-related expenses.

Do families have to purchase textbooks and other course materials?

Textbooks and other course materials cost approximately $500 - $750 per year. For families receiving financial aid, proportional aid is provided for textbooks and other course materials.

What about athletic team apparel?

Athletic team gear costs approximately $100 per sport. For families receiving financial aid, proportional aid is provided for athletic team gear and equipment.

Does Newark Academy offer ‘no need’ or ‘Merit Based Scholarships’?

All financial aid at Newark Academy is need-based. NA does not offer merit-based scholarships.
