Where the First Draft of Extraordinary is Written

At Westridge we cultivate the extraordinary in every girl by helping them to develop the confidence, skills, and values to thrive wherever life takes them.

Mobile

Infinite Space for Unstoppable Girls

Westridge is a place where girls are the most powerful voices in every room, the most celebrated athletes, the most courageous, compassionate leaders. It’s a community where girls are encouraged to take chances, make mistakes, think deeply, and get involved—and they do.

Discover the westridge difference

Discover Your Origin Story at Westridge

On our campus girls feel a sense of ownership over their academic journey and they experience the right balance of challenge, freedom, and support to forge their own remarkable trajectory in school and in life. 

Explore our Curriculum

Over a Century of  Never Standing Still

Since 1913, Westridge has empowered generations of girls to challenge the status quo, improve their school and the world around them, and look for ways to lead and serve their community. But our forward-thinking nature also extends to our present: we're not just readying girls for the future—we're empowering and educating them to shape it.

Advanced Coursework

Unique to Westridge and designed to go beyond standardized Advanced Placement classes, Westridge Advanced Courses provide meaningful academic challenge that engages student interest, cultivates conceptual and critical thinking, and inspires deep learning and knowledge creation. We’re redefining rigor in the best way.

Learn More

Visual Arts

Visual Arts at Westridge foster curious, confident, vibrant art makers. Students learn the powerful role of art in society through their own creative discovery, developing technical proficiency, and implementing formal art concepts in a variety of media, from painting and ceramics to photography and 3D printing.

Learn More

Athletics

Tigers are fierce and courageous, and learn the values of discipline, dedication, selflessness, and hard work through athletics, which begin with recreational tournaments in Lower School and expand to 10 competitive sports in Middle School and 13 in Upper School.

Learn More

STEM at Westridge

At Westridge, girls see themselves as the most capable scientists, engineers, programmers, and mathematicians—and they are! Beginning with a STEM course in 4th grade, our students have a broad and deep array of STEM core and elective courses to choose from—including four years of computer science and four years of aerospace engineering in the Upper School.
