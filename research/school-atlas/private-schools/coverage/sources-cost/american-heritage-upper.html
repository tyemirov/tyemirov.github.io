Tuition, Aid & Fees

 

American Heritage Schools operates exclusively on tuition and fees, proudly maintaining a policy of not soliciting or accepting any donations or charitable contributions. This unique policy ensures all expenses are covered without external funding or fundraising.

American Heritage schools Tuition 2027 - 2028
PK3 - PK4 $37,900*
Kindergarten $40,300**
1st - 5th Grade $43,400**
6th - 8th Grade $46,400**
9th - 12th Grade $49,800**

Our schools accept state-funded Family Empowerment Scholarships (UA, EO, FTC) through Step Up for Students, which provide an average of $8,000 per child, per year - available to families regardless of income. Learn more at stepupforstudents.org.

PAYMENT PLANS:

A. One (1) Payment: Pay the full tuition amount by March 1.

B. Two (2) Installments: Make two equal payments, with the first due by April 1 and the second by December 1.

C. Twelve (12) Monthly Installments: Spread the tuition over twelve equal payments, due on the first day of each month from May 1 through April 1 of the following year. A 6% surcharge to tuition will be added.


*Includes complimentary lunch
**Includes complimentary express bus to selected areas
